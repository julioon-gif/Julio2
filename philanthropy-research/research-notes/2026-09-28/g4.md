## REI (Recreational Equipment, Inc.)

- **HQ:** Kent, WA (per brief; HQ not re-verified this session)
- **Giving programs:**
  - REI Cooperative Action Fund (REI Fund): a separate nonprofit, national. https://www.reifund.org/
  - REI Fund Local Grant Program: REI stores and distribution centers recommend local nonprofits (568 recommendations in one cycle). https://grantedai.com/grants/rei-cooperative-action-fund-grants-rei-cooperative-action-fund-0163c357
  - Corporate philanthropic donations and sponsorships go through REI's government and community relations team (a team email address is listed on REI's contact page). https://www.rei.com/about-rei/contact-us
- **Focus areas:** Making the outdoors accessible and inclusive, under three initiatives: Creating Space Outside, Connecting People Outside, and **Centering Health Outside** (nature and health). https://www.reifund.org/grantees/approach ; https://www.reifund.org/grantees/health
- **Typical grant size:** Spring 2026 cohort was 44 grants totaling $3.4M. Listed grants are at least $50,000 each, mostly unrestricted and multiyear. https://www.rei.com/newsroom/article/rei-cooperative-action-fund-spring-2026-grantees ; https://www.reifund.org/newestgrantees/2026-spring
- **Geographic limits:** National, plus local through store recommendations.
- **How to apply:** **Invitation only.** There are two cycles a year (spring and fall). https://grantedai.com/grants/rei-cooperative-action-fund-grants-rei-cooperative-action-fund-0163c357 ; https://www.reifund.org/faqs
- **Open RFPs/deadlines:** None. Invitation only.
- **Past cancer/ACS gifts:** None found.
- **Employee giving/matching:** Employees can give to the REI Fund, with workplace matching lookup at reifund.org/donate. REI matched #OptOutside donations to the REI Fund up to $1M. No general REI employee match for outside charities was confirmed (unverified). https://www.reifund.org/donate/employee ; https://www.rei.com/newsroom/article/optoutside-rei-co-op-to-match-donations-to-the-rei-cooperative-action-fund-up-to-1-million
- **Grants/contact URL:** https://www.reifund.org/ ; https://www.rei.com/about-rei/contact-us
- **Suggested Tier:** 3 (long shot)
- **ACS angle:** The REI Fund's "Centering Health Outside" initiative opens a narrow door for survivorship and outdoor-wellness programming. Otherwise the best angle is an in-kind or sponsorship request to a local REI store for a Relay For Life or Making Strides event.

- **Geographic limits:** Communities where associates live and work, starting with Seattle (unverified detail)

## Columbia Bank / Columbia Banking System (Umpqua Bank Charitable Foundation / Columbia Bank Community Impact Fund)

- **HQ:** Tacoma, WA (per brief). Columbia Banking System is the parent of Umpqua Bank, and the combined bank now operates as Columbia Bank. https://www.columbiabank.com/newsroom/umpqua-bank-and-parent-company-columbia-banking-system-celebrate-anniversaries-with-$1m-in-grants-for-local-nonprofits/
- **Giving programs:** Umpqua Bank Charitable Foundation community grants, renamed and restructured as the **Columbia Bank Community Impact Fund** starting in 2026. Also the Connect Volunteer Network. https://www.columbiabank.com/our-impact/partnerships/
  - **Not to be confused with** the Columbia Bank Foundation of Fair Lawn, NJ. The micro and community grant structure in the brief belongs to that New Jersey foundation. See the correction at the top.
- **Focus areas (2026):** Financial Mobility, Housing Stability, and Community Resilience. Community Resilience covers education, workforce development and essential services that support economic participation. Health is not a named priority. https://www.columbiabank.com/our-impact/partnerships/
- **Typical grant size:** 2024 totals were $1.25M to 212 nonprofits, and one 2024 cycle gave 78 grants totaling $456,500 (about $5.9K average). An earlier 2023 cycle gave 78 grants totaling $417K. 2026 amounts are unverified. https://www.columbiabank.com/blog/umpqua-bank-charitable-foundation-awards-grants-to-78-nonprofits-to-close-out-2024-community-grants-program/ ; https://www.columbiabankingsystem.com/news-market-data/press-releases/press-release/2023/Umpqua-Bank-Charitable-Foundation-Awards-Community-Grants-to-78-Nonprofits/default.aspx
- **Geographic limits:** Communities with Columbia/Umpqua branches and associates (OR, WA, CA, ID, NV and others). https://grantexec.com/grants/c9751483-724e-4622-bac3-ee92d54997b1
- **How to apply:** **Invitation only starting in 2026.** The fund does not accept unsolicited requests or LOIs and has no open cycles. Eligibility requires current Columbia Bank associate volunteer engagement plus focus-area alignment. https://www.columbiabank.com/our-impact/partnerships/
- **Open RFPs/deadlines:** None open. An aggregator lists a June 10, 2026 deadline, apparently for invited applicants only. That date has passed and is unverified. https://grantexec.com/grants/c9751483-724e-4622-bac3-ee92d54997b1
- **Past cancer/ACS gifts:** None found. https://www.columbiabank.com/globalassets/media/documents/2025_community_services_overview.pdf (no ACS mention in the snippet)
- **Employee giving/matching:** Associates get up to 40 hours of paid volunteer time. Logging 40 hours in a year earns a $500 donation to a nonprofit of the associate's choice. The Community Impact Fund offers every associate a $1,000 matching gift. https://www.columbiabank.com/our-impact/partnerships/volunteer/ ; https://www.columbiabank.com/our-impact/partnerships/
- **Grants/contact URL:** https://www.columbiabank.com/our-impact/partnerships/
- **Suggested Tier:** 3
- **ACS angle:** Grant access requires associate volunteer engagement, so first recruit Columbia Bank associates as Relay For Life, Making Strides or Road to Recovery volunteers. Their volunteer hours ($500) and $1,000 gift match can then flow to ACS. Any grant ask would need framing as "Community Resilience / essential services," such as rides to treatment that keep patients employed.
