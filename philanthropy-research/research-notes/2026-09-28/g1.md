## Costco
- **HQ:** Issaquah, WA
- **Giving program(s):** Costco Wholesale Charitable Contributions program (corporate giving, grants through a Benevity online application). There is also a local Warehouse Donation Request (paper form handed in at the nearest warehouse, usually Costco Shop Cards). The Costco Foundation is a separate 501(c)(3), EIN 91-1799391; I could not see its 990 details because ProPublica was blocked. Nationally, Costco runs a workplace giving campaign with United Way and a partnership with Children's Miracle Network Hospitals. Sources: https://costco.com/charitable-giving.html ; https://www.costco.com/sustainability-communities.html ; https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/organizations/911799391
- **Focus areas:** children, education, and health and human services. Costco accepts monetary requests only. Costco budgets 1% of pretax profits for charitable giving, and its Charitable Contributions Committee meets quarterly. Sources: https://costco.com/charitable-giving.html ; https://www.costco.com/f/-/my-life-commitment-to-communities
- **Exclusions (important for ACS):** Costco does **not** fund transportation and travel expenses, **research studies**, athletic teams, events or **sponsorships**, capital or equipment, salaries or staff training, general awareness or advocacy campaigns, merchandise for auctions, conferences, or parties and celebrations. These exclusions come from third-party summaries of Costco's guidelines and should be confirmed on costco.com. Sources: https://grantable.co/grants/costco-wholesale-charitable-contributions-grant-a31725862c19 ; https://www.instrumentl.com/grants/costco-wholesale-grants
- **Typical grant size:** not published. The guidance is to ask for no more than 10% of the program budget and to keep a first request conservative. Dollar range: unverified. Sources: https://charitysafaris.com/costco-donation-request-tips/ ; https://www.theshareway.com/blog/costco-donation-request
- **Geographic limits:** communities where Costco operates warehouses (a local-community focus). A formal PNW-only limit is unverified. Source: https://costco.com/charitable-giving.html
- **How to apply:** open application, rolling. Grants go through the Benevity online application and warehouse gifts through the paper Warehouse Donation Request form. Review takes 4–6 weeks. Allow 3 months' lead time before a dated event. Typically one request per fiscal year. Costco weighs identified community need, measurable outcomes, collaboration, a broad funding base and the project budget. Sources: https://costco.com/charitable-giving-faq.html ; https://www.costco.com/wcsstore/CostcoUSBCCatalogAssetStore/Attachment/Warehouse-Donation-Request-093013.pdf ; https://www.theshareway.com/blog/costco-donation-request
- **Open RFPs / deadlines:** none; applications are rolling. There is no dated cycle.
- **Past cancer / ACS giving:** none found. A search for Costco + ACS / Relay For Life / Making Strides returned nothing. Costco raised $54M for Children's Miracle Network Hospitals in FY2025 and more than $550M since 1988. Some CMN hospitals treat pediatric cancer, but this is not ACS. Total charitable giving was reported at more than $97M in FY2025 (per the sustainability report summary). Sources: https://costcowholesalecorp.childrensmiraclenetworkhospitals.org/ ; https://www.sustainabilityreports.com/costco-wholesale/2025/annual-sustainability-report
- **Employee giving / matching:** the Workplace Giving Campaign (employee donations plus a company match, run with United Way) has raised $509M over 40+ years. Match terms are unverified. Source: https://www.costco.com/sustainability-communities.html
- **Grants / contact page:** https://costco.com/charitable-giving.html ; FAQ https://costco.com/charitable-giving-faq.html ; generic grant inbox CommunityRelations@costco.com (reported in search-result summaries; unverified on costco.com)
- **Suggested Tier:** 2
- **ACS angle:** Pitch a health-and-human-services program for patients in Costco's home communities, such as **Hope Lodge lodging nights or the 24/7 helpline/navigation**, not Road to Recovery (Costco excludes transportation and travel) and not research or event sponsorship (both excluded). Keep the ask to 10% or less of the program budget.
- **Change vs. last week:** the ACS angle should move away from Road to Recovery rides because of the transportation/travel exclusion. The "sponsorship" and "research studies" exclusions also rule out Relay For Life or gala asks.

## BECU
- **HQ:** Tukwila, WA
- **Giving program(s):** BECU Foundation People Helping People Awards (member-nominated grants); BECU Community Giving / strategic financial-health partnerships; BECU sponsorships (community events); BECU Cares (employee matching and volunteer rewards); BECU Foundation Scholarships. Sources: https://www.becu.org/members-matter/community-involvement/people-helping-people ; https://www.becu.org/members-matter/community-involvement/community-giving ; https://sponsorshiprequest.becu.org/en ; https://www.becu.org/members-matter/community-involvement/employee-inspired-giving
- **Focus areas:** one People Helping People category covers health: "preserving health and promoting wellness, including access to healthcare, illness prevention/cure, mental health, patient support, disabilities, and veteran advocacy". The strategic Community Giving program focuses on financial health: postsecondary and financial education, workforce skills, entrepreneurism and homeownership. Sources: https://www.becu.org/members-matter/community-involvement/people-helping-people ; https://www.becu.org/members-matter/community-involvement/community-giving
- **Typical grant size (People Helping People):** at least fourteen $15,000 Community Benefit Awards; one $50,000 Member Volunteer of the Year; one $40,000 People's Choice; one $30,000 Past Recipients' Choice; one $30,000 Employees' Choice. About $450,000 was distributed in the 2025 cycle (announced December 2025). Sources: https://www.becu.org/members-matter/community-involvement/people-helping-people ; https://gowestassociation.org/2025/12/17/becu-members-help-direct-450000-to-nonprofits-through-people-helping-people-awards/
- **Geographic limits:** active 501(c)(3)s with direct impact in **WA, OR, ID** or SC. Sponsorships are limited to the same states. Sources: https://www.instrumentl.com/grants/becu-foundation-people-helping-people-awards ; https://sponsorshiprequest.becu.org/en
- **How to apply:** People Helping People works by **BECU member nomination**, and the nominee may then be asked to complete an application. Community Giving partnerships are **identified and sought out by BECU's philanthropy team** (no unsolicited route). Sponsorship requests are open year-round, with a response within 60 days, but BECU does **not** fund fundraising events (breakfasts, luncheons, galas, auctions), so Relay For Life, Making Strides and galas are likely ineligible. Sources: https://www.becu.org/members-matter/community-involvement/community-giving ; https://sponsorshiprequest.becu.org/en
- **Open RFPs / deadlines:** 2026 nominations are closed, and finalists and People's Choice voting are expected in October 2026. The 2027 nomination window is not published (unverified). Past windows ran mid-June to early July (for example June 14 to July 2 in 2021), so the 2027 window will likely be around June 2027, but this is unverified. Sources: https://www.becu.org/members-matter/community-involvement/people-helping-people ; https://thesubtimes.com/2021/06/15/becus-2021-people-helping-people-awards-nominations-are-open/
- **Past cancer giving:** 2021 People Helping People awards went to **Cancer Lifeline** ($15,000 Community Benefit Award) and **Camp Agape Northwest** (a camp for kids with cancer and their families; Past Recipients' Choice winner). No ACS gift was found. Source: https://newsroom.becu.org/People-Helping-People-Awards-BECU-Grants-535,000-to-Member-Nominated-Nonprofits
- **Employee giving / matching:** BECU Cares matches up to **$7,500 per employee per year** to eligible 501(c)(3)s, with matches also for retirees and board members. Its Volunteer Rewards include team volunteering (5+ coworkers). Members' gifts are not matched. Source: https://doublethedonation.com/matching-gifts/becu-boeing-employees-credit-union (third-party; confirm at https://www.becu.org/members-matter/community-involvement/employee-inspired-giving)
- **Grants / contact page:** https://www.becu.org/members-matter/community-involvement/giving-and-scholarships ; generic foundation inbox becu_foundation@becu.org (from search-result summary; unverified on becu.org)
- **Suggested Tier:** 1
- **ACS angle:** People Helping People names "patient support" and "illness prevention/cure", and BECU has funded cancer-support nonprofits before (Cancer Lifeline, Camp Agape). Recruit ACS volunteers who are BECU members to nominate ACS's WA/OR/ID Road to Recovery or patient navigation work in the 2027 window.
