## Google
- **HQ:** Mountain View, CA. PNW footprint: offices in Kirkland and Seattle, WA, and a data center in The Dalles, OR (https://datacenters.google/locations/oregon/). The Kirkland/Seattle office details are unverified this session.
- **Giving programs:**
  - National: Google.org, the company's philanthropic arm.
  - PNW: community grants and investment tied to The Dalles data center. Google.org has given more than $14M in grants to Oregon organizations since 2009. Google and Googlers have given more than $35M to Oregon nonprofits since 2004. (https://datacenters.google/locations/oregon/)
- **Focus areas:** Google.org gives mainly to AI for social good, economic opportunity/digital skills, crisis response and science (general knowledge, unverified this session). In The Dalles it funds community infrastructure (water and sewer), digital skills through Grow with Google (124,000+ Oregonians trained) and local schools and community groups (https://datacenters.google/locations/oregon/). The City of The Dalles surveyed residents on how to use Google-provided funds (https://www.columbiagorgenews.com/news/your-voice-matters-the-dalles-surveying-residents-on-use-of-google-funds/article_5966382a-2f5e-11ef-bd20-3ffa9f0b34f2.html).
- **Typical grant size:** Unverified for local Dalles-area community grants.
- **Geographic limits:** Data-center community grants go to Wasco County / The Dalles. Google.org is national and global.
- **How to apply:** Google.org mostly funds by invitation or through its own challenges (unverified). No open application process for The Dalles community grants was confirmed. Start with the data-center community page: https://datacenters.google/locations/oregon/
- **Open RFPs/deadlines:** None found.
- **Past cancer/ACS giving:** None found (unverified).
- **Employee giving/matching:** Google runs an employee donation-matching program (details unverified).
- **Contact page:** https://datacenters.google/locations/oregon/ ; https://www.google.org/
- **Suggested tier:** 3
- **ACS angle:** A Google Dalles community grant could fund Road to Recovery rides for rural Gorge patients who travel to Portland for treatment. A pitch to Google.org would need a technology or AI angle, such as the ACS 24/7 helpline and digital navigation.

## Meta
- **HQ:** Menlo Park, CA. PNW footprint: offices in Seattle and Bellevue, WA (unverified this session), a data center in Prineville, OR, and a data center in Kuna, ID that came online in September 2026 (https://datacenters.atmeta.com/2026/09/kuna-we-are-online/).
- **Giving programs:**
  - PNW: Meta Data Center Community Action Grants, which cover Prineville/Crook County and Kuna/Ada County (https://datacenters.atmeta.com/community-action-grants/). Meta gave the Kuna School District $200,000. Other Kuna-area grantees include the Boise Metro Chamber, Boys & Girls Clubs of Ada County and College of Western Idaho (https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/metas-data-center-grants-fueling-innovation-in-local-communities/).
  - National: Facebook/Meta fundraising tools with GivingTuesday and recurring-donation matches. Meta matches recurring donations up to $100 per donation and $100,000 per nonprofit, with a $7M total (https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/Meta-to-Match-up-to-7-Million-in-Charitable-Donations-GivingTuesday/635531/).
- **Focus areas:** Community Action Grants fund technology for community benefit, building strong and sustainable communities, and STEAM education (https://ktvz.com/news/crook-county/2025/10/17/crook-county-urges-organizations-apply-for-meta-data-center-community-action-grants/). The 2026 cycle added AI and workforce development (https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/metas-data-center-grants-fueling-innovation-in-local-communities/).
- **Typical grant size:** Unverified for the typical award. The largest sourced example is $200K to Kuna SD. Meta made 328 grants in the 2026 cycle (https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/...).
- **Geographic limits:** Only communities where Meta has a data center.
- **How to apply:** Open application through ChangeX. Nonprofits may submit one application per cycle. Projects need metrics that can be reported publicly, and funds must be spent within one year (https://datacenters.atmeta.com/community-action-grants/ ; https://www.changex.org/us/funds/Meta-PrinevilleDataCenter).
- **Open RFPs/deadlines:** The 2027 cycle opened 2026-09-14 and closes **2026-10-19** (search-result text for the pages above; page blocked, so confirm). The previous cycle closed 2025-11-21 and announced awards in spring 2026 (https://opportunitydesk.org/2025/10/21/meta-data-center-community-action-grants-2026/).
- **Past cancer/ACS giving:** None found. Meta's Facebook fundraiser tools are used for ACS peer fundraising, but no formal partnership was confirmed.
- **Employee giving/matching:** Meta has an employee matching-gift program, but the ratio and cap are unverified (https://doublethedonation.com/matching-gifts/meta-previously-facebook was blocked).
- **Contact page:** https://datacenters.atmeta.com/community-action-grants/
- **Suggested tier:** 2
- **ACS angle:** Apply before 2026-10-19 with a technology-framed project for Crook County (Prineville) or Ada County (Kuna). One option is digital patient navigation plus Road to Recovery ride scheduling for rural Central Oregon patients who travel to Bend or Portland. Another is a STEAM/cancer-prevention youth education project.

## Bristol Myers Squibb (BMS)
- **HQ:** Princeton, NJ. The HQ city is from general knowledge and unverified this session. PNW footprint: about 1,000 employees at a Seattle South Lake Union R&D site (ex-Juno) and a cell-therapy manufacturing facility in Bothell, WA (https://www.bms.com/our-company/locations/washington.html ; https://www.425business.com/news/bothell-facility-at-forefront-of-personalized-cell-therapy/article_b9a354cb-7357-5106-aab9-9bd44fb43bba.html).
- **Giving programs:**
  - National: the Bristol Myers Squibb Foundation. Its cancer programs include Bridging Cancer Care and survivorship programs, focused mainly on the states with the highest lung-cancer incidence.
  - Local, where employees live and work (including WA): the BMS corporate Community Giving Program. Sponsorships go through Grants, Giving & Corporate Sponsorship Support.
  - Sources: https://www.bms.com/about-us/responsibility/bristol-myers-squibb-foundation/our-focus-areas/cancer.html ; https://www.bms.com/about-us/responsibility/corporate-giving.html
- **Focus areas:** The Foundation covers cancer (screening and survivorship equity), pediatric blood disorders, neuropsychiatry (added 2026) and diversity in clinical trials (https://www.bms.com/our-impact/bristol-myers-squibb-foundation.html). Community Giving covers unmet medical needs, STEM education and essential services (https://grantedai.com/grants/community-giving-program-bristol-myers-squibb-foundation-inc-b7f97dde).
- **Typical grant size:** Foundation cancer grants run about $1M or more over multiple years. Example: ACS received $1.25M over 3 years (https://www.pharmacytimes.com/view/bristol-myers-squibb-foundation-awards-eight-grants-totaling-nearly-115m-to-make-lung-and-skin-cancer-screening-care-more-accessible-in-high-risk-communities). Community Giving amounts are unverified.
- **Geographic limits:** Community Giving serves US communities where BMS employees live and work, which includes the Seattle/Bothell area. Foundation cancer grants focus on high-burden states, mostly in the Southeast.
- **How to apply:** Community Giving takes online applications through CyberGrants year-round. Submit at least 8 weeks before funds are needed (https://www.bms.com/our-impact/corporate-giving/how-to-apply.html). Foundation grants appear to be by invitation or partnership (unverified). Also check the current funding opportunities page: https://www.bms.com/about-us/responsibility/corporate-giving/current-funding-opportunities.html
- **Open RFPs/deadlines:** None sourced. Community Giving is rolling.
- **Past cancer/ACS giving:** Strong existing national relationship.
  - A $1.25M, 3-year grant to ACS for lung-cancer screening navigation with three FQHCs (https://www.biospace.com/b-bristol-myers-squibb-foundation-b-awards-eight-grants-totaling-nearly-11-5-million-to-make-lung-and-skin-cancer-screening-care-more-accessible).
  - The ACS–BMSF Comprehensive Lung Cancer Patient Support Program (CLCPSP) in GA, AL, MS, WV, SC, NC, KY and TN (https://www.bms.com/about-us/responsibility/bristol-myers-squibb-foundation/our-focus-areas/cancer/american-cancer-society-clcpsp.html).
  - Survivorship care models funded as part of the Cancer Moonshot (https://www.biospace.com/b-bristol-myers-squibb-foundation-b-joins-white-house-cancer-moonshot).
  - ACS was a grantee under BMS's $150M health-equity pledge (https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/bristol-myers-squibb-announces-nearly-8-million-new-grants-to-address-health-equity).
  - No Seattle-area ACS event sponsorship was found (unverified).
- **Employee giving/matching:** The Foundation has a matching-gift program, but the ratio and cap are unverified. The Helping Hands program gives employees paid volunteer days (https://www.bms.com/about-us/responsibility/supporting-our-communities.html).
- **Contact page:** https://www.bms.com/our-impact/corporate-giving/how-to-apply.html
- **Suggested tier:** 1
- **ACS angle:** BMS is already a national ACS lung-cancer partner. Locally, ask the Seattle/Bothell cell-therapy site through Community Giving to sponsor Making Strides / Relay or fund Road to Recovery and Hope Lodge-style lodging support for cell-therapy (CAR-T) patients, who must stay near their Seattle treatment center.
