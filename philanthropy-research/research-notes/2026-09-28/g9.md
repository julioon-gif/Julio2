## McKinstry
- **HQ:** Seattle, WA
- **Foundation / program:** McKinstry Charitable Foundation (private foundation, EIN 91-1942024, est. 1998). The company's giving and volunteer programs run under "Building Good." Sources: https://www.causeiq.com/organizations/mckinstry-charitable-foundation,911942024/ ; https://www.mckinstry.com/who-we-are/building-good/
- **Focus areas:** education and STEM, youth and family services, conservation and environment, food security, animal welfare, racial justice, **health and science**, athletics, and economic initiatives. Also housing and services for homeless and vulnerable people, especially youth. Recent emphasis on climate: a $1M commitment to climate nonprofits under its "Action for Impact" strategy, finished with $520K to 8 climate nonprofits. Sources: https://www.insidephilanthropy.com/find-a-grant-places/washington-grants/mckinstry-charitable-foundation ; https://www.mckinstry.com/all-features/mckinstry-advances-climate-commitment-with-1-million-in-climate-focused-charitable-awards/ (the announcement date shown in search results was May 2026, **unverified**)
- **Typical grant size:** 2023: $1,497,050 in total grants across 332 grants. Range $100 to $700,000, and most grants are small (average about $4.5K, calculated from those figures). Sources: https://www.insidephilanthropy.com/find-a-grant-places/washington-grants/mckinstry-charitable-foundation ; https://www.instrumentl.com/990-report/mckinstry-charitable-foundation
- **Geographic limits:** gives nationally where McKinstry operates, with most funding in WA (about $2M), then CO (about $148K) and OR (about $141K) (Instrumentl / Inside Philanthropy aggregation; years **unverified**). The 2025 Social Impact Report cites more than $1.5M to 320 nonprofits nationwide. Source: https://www.mckinstry.com/news-insights/mckinstry-releases-2025-social-impact-report/
- **How to apply:** **invitation only.** The foundation does not accept applications; Inside Philanthropy advises getting an introduction through an employee or past grantee. Source: https://www.insidephilanthropy.com/find-a-grant-places/washington-grants/mckinstry-charitable-foundation
- **Open RFPs / deadlines:** none found.
- **Past cancer / ACS giving:** none found in public sources. ACS account history should be checked in the CRM. The one "McKinstry + Relay" search hit was unrelated (a person named McKinstry at UNM).
- **Employee giving / matching:** an employee matching and volunteer program; an "Impact Fund" matches dollars for employees' community service time (details **unverified**). Benefits include wellness programs and on-site gyms. Sources: https://www.insidephilanthropy.com/find-a-grant-places/washington-grants/mckinstry-charitable-foundation ; https://www.mckinstry.com/careers/benefits/
- **Leadership:** Dean Allen is Chairman and CEO. No CEO change found; corporate officer promotions were announced in about December 2024. Sources: https://www.mckinstry.com/leadership/dean-allen/ ; https://www.mckinstry.com/news/mckinstry-announces-key-corporate-officer-promotions-to-bolster-leadership-team/
- **Grants / contact page:** https://www.mckinstry.com/who-we-are/building-good/
- **Suggested Tier:** 2
- **Expansion angle:** "Health and science" and "vulnerable people" are listed priorities, and McKinstry has a large field workforce. Pitch a workforce-wellness partnership (construction-trades cancer screening, tobacco cessation, sun safety) alongside an employee match/Impact Fund push around Relay or Making Strides. Use existing McKinstry-employee ACS volunteers as the internal door, since grants are by invitation.

## PCC Community Markets
- **HQ:** Seattle, WA (member-owned food co-op; about $488M net sales per Grocery Dive). Source: https://www.grocerydive.com/news/pcc-community-markets-president-ceo-krish-srinivasan-retire/828380/
- **Foundation / program:** Store Donations program (in-kind items and gift cards); PCC Food Bank Program (register round-up, "donate the difference"); food-security rapid-response campaign (2025). Sources: https://www.pccmarkets.com/sustainability/donations/ ; https://www.pccmarkets.com/sustainability/food-bank-program/ ; https://www.pccmarkets.com/news/2025/food-security-initiative/
- **Focus areas:** "the nexus of food, equity and sustainability"; hunger relief (more than 40 food bank and nonprofit partners). Source: https://www.pccmarkets.com/sustainability/donations/guidelines/
- **Typical grant size:** store donations are **capped at $100 in value per request** (in kind or gift card). No cash grant program found. Source: https://www.pccmarkets.com/sustainability/donations/guidelines/
- **Geographic limits:** local 501(c)(3)s and public agencies near a PCC store (Puget Sound). Apply to one neighborhood store only. Source: same as above.
- **How to apply:** open online form; requests due at least 30 days ahead; items picked up in person at the store. Source: https://www.pccmarkets.com/sustainability/donations/request-form/
- **Open RFPs / deadlines:** none (rolling).
- **Past cancer / ACS giving:** none found publicly.
- **Employee giving / matching:** none found. Workforce is unionized; Grocery Dive notes improved compensation and benefits under the current CEO. Source: https://www.grocerydive.com/news/pcc-community-markets-president-ceo-krish-srinivasan-retire/828380/
- **Leadership / growth:** CEO Krish Srinivasan retires effective **Jan 29, 2027**, and a national search is under way (board chair Joe Rogoff is leading it). The new Madison Valley store (17th) opens Oct 14, 2026. Sources: https://www.grocerydive.com/news/pcc-community-markets-president-ceo-krish-srinivasan-retire/828380/ ; https://www.425business.com/news/pcc-community-markets-ceo-to-retire/article_09661b86-cb39-445c-bcb5-060c4452fa93.html ; https://www.pccmarkets.com/news/2026/madison-valley-opening-announced/
- **Grants / contact page:** https://www.pccmarkets.com/sustainability/donations/
- **Suggested Tier:** 2 (nutrition and cancer-prevention tie; small cash capacity)
- **Expansion angle:** Propose a cause-marketing or register round-up month tied to nutrition-based cancer prevention (for example, colorectal screening awareness in March), plus in-kind healthy food for Hope Lodge guests. Time relationship-building to the CEO transition, since the new leader may reset community priorities in 2027.
