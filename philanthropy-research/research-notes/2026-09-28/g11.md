## CareOregon – Community Giving and the CareOregon Foundation
- **HQ:** Portland, OR (a Medicaid CCO/health plan).
- **Foundation/program:** CareOregon Community Giving (grants and sponsorships), plus a new 2026 CareOregon Foundation grant opportunity.
- **Focus areas:** health equity and social determinants of health. The 2026 priorities are (1) food and nutrition education, (2) housing support, and (3) **access to care and navigation services** (culturally specific navigation, health literacy, primary care engagement). Grantees must serve Medicaid or low-income populations in Clackamas, Clatsop, Columbia, Jackson, Multnomah, Tillamook and/or Washington counties.

- **Deadlines:** the 2026 Foundation LOI window was June 8–26 (year per snippet context, so confirm). It has passed. The next cycle is unverified.
- **Cancer/ACS ties:** none found.
- **Suggested Tier:** **2**
- **ACS angle:** patient navigation and health literacy for Medicaid patients (cancer screening navigation, CHW-led outreach), and Road to Recovery in the Portland metro and Jackson County.
