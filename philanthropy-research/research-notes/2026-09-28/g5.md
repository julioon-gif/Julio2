## Cambia Health Solutions / Cambia Health Foundation / Regence
- **HQ:** Portland, OR
- **Giving programs:** Cambia Health Foundation (corporate foundation, EIN 32-0200578), which covers OR, WA, ID and UT. Regence BlueShield (WA), Regence BlueCross BlueShield of Oregon and Regence BlueShield of Idaho also make community investments and sponsorships. Sources: https://www.cambiahealth.com/what-we-do/community-impact/ ; https://news.regence.com/releases/regence-impacts-community-health-through-nonprofit-partnerships ; https://www.hinchilla.com/funders-us/320200578-cambia-health-foundation
- **Focus areas:** Under the 2022–2026 plan: Resilient Children and Families, Healthy and Connected Aging, and Health Care Workforce (integrated primary care and the behavioral health workforce). The foundation also runs the Sojourns palliative care program for people with serious illness and their caregivers. Regence's community focus areas include addiction prevention, supporting seniors and sustainability. Sources: https://www.cambiahealthfoundation.org/focus-areas/health-care-workforce.html ; https://www.cambiahealthfoundation.org/focus-areas/sojourns-grants.html ; https://www.gih.org/grantmaker-focus/cambia-health-foundation/ ; https://news.regence.com/releases/regence-impacts-community-health-through-nonprofit-partnerships
- **Typical grant size:** $10,000–$100,000. Source: https://www.grantable.co/search/funders/profile/cambia-health-foundation-us-foundation-320200578 (third-party). Recent example: $1.2M across several grants (Dec 2025), https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251215593882/en/Cambia-Health-Foundation-Invests-$1.2-Million-to-Advance-Whole-Person-Health ; $280K to support older-adult kinship caregivers (May 2025), https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250513144548/en/
- **Geographic limits:** Prioritizes Idaho, Oregon, Utah and Washington. Source: https://www.cambiahealthfoundation.org/
- **How to apply:** Periodic RFPs submitted through the applicant portal. Otherwise invitation-based. Sign up for email updates. Source: https://www.cambiahealthfoundation.org/applicant-resources/current-funding-opportunities.html
- **Open RFPs and deadlines:** None confirmed. The strategic plan ends in 2026, and no 2027 plan was found in search (unverified).
- **Past cancer/ACS ties:** **Regence BlueShield received the ACS Corporate Impact Award for excellence in cancer control, the highest honor ACS gives a company.** Regence's events and sponsorships manager has sat on the local ACS board (date unverified, person not named here). Source: https://news.regence.com/blog/regence-blueshield-a-leading-force-in-the-fight-against-cancer (search-result summary; the page was blocked). No Cambia Health Foundation grant to ACS was found.
- **Employee giving:** The foundation matches employee gifts at 50%, up to a $10,000 match per employee. Dollars for Doers pays $20 per volunteer hour. The annual "Celebration of Giving" campaign raised $2.5M for 2,492 causes in 2024. Sources: https://doublethedonation.com/matching-gifts/cambia-health-solutions ; https://www.cambiahealth.com/news-and-stories/pulse-blog/celebration-giving-harnessing-power-good-through-employee-giving ; https://www.cambiahealth.com/news-and-stories/pulse-blog/cambia-employees-raised-over-500000-during-2023-celebration-giving
- **Grants/contact page:** https://www.cambiahealthfoundation.org/applicant-resources/current-funding-opportunities.html
- **Suggested Tier:** **1**
- **ACS angle:** Regence already has an ACS Corporate Impact Award relationship. Reopen it through Regence's sponsorships team (Relay For Life, Making Strides, and screening outreach to members). For the foundation, frame ACS navigation and Road to Recovery as whole-person support for older adults and caregivers (Healthy and Connected Aging and the Sojourns serious-illness program).

## Columbia Sportswear
- **HQ:** Portland, OR
- **Giving programs:** Columbia Sportswear Company Charitable Contributions Program, run by Community Relations. The Columbia/Grassroots Outdoor Alliance "Belay" program funds conservation and outdoor education. Sources: https://fconline.foundationcenter.org/fdo-grantmaker-profile/?collection=grantmakers&key=COLU195 ; https://investor.columbia.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/180/columbiagrassroots-outdoor-alliance-announce-2016-belay
- **Focus areas:** Conservation, youth and diversity, product initiatives, and community investment. The corporate responsibility pillars are Empowering People, Sustaining Places and Responsible Practices. Sources: Candid profile above ; https://www.columbiasportswear.ca/en/csr-people/ ; https://investor.columbia.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/351/columbia-sportswear-company-releases-2022-impact-report
- **Typical grant size:** unverified
- **Geographic limits:** Communities where it employs people, manufactures, sources or does business. Source: https://www.columbiasportswear.ca/en/csr-people/
- **How to apply:** **Does not accept unsolicited grant or sponsorship requests.** Grantees must be 501(c)(3)s. Questions go to Community Relations. Source: https://fconline.foundationcenter.org/fdo-grantmaker-profile/?collection=grantmakers&key=COLU195
- **Open RFPs and deadlines:** None.
- **Past cancer/ACS ties:** No corporate ACS gift found. Leadership has given large personal cancer gifts: Gert Boyle gave $100M to the OHSU Knight Cancer Challenge (2014), and CEO Tim and Mary Boyle gave $10M to OHSU/UO for cancer data science (2020). These are personal or family gifts, not corporate ones. Source: https://www.ohsu.edu/knight-cancer-institute/knight-cancer-institute-mission-and-milestones
- **Employee giving:** 1:1 match, up to $1,000 per employee a year, $25 minimum. Sometimes 2:1 during campaigns. Source: https://doublethedonation.com/matching-gifts/columbia-sportswear-company (third-party)
- **Grants/contact page:** https://www.columbiasportswear.ca/en/csr-people/ (Community Relations; no public application page)
- **Suggested Tier:** **3**
- **ACS angle:** Corporate giving is conservation and outdoors-focused and invitation-only. The realistic routes are an employee Relay For Life or Making Strides team with the 1:1 match, or a sun-safety/skin-cancer prevention tie-in with Columbia's outdoor apparel (e.g., UPF gear). The Boyle family's cancer philanthropy is a separate major-gifts lead, not a corporate one.
