## Bank of America

- **HQ:** Charlotte, NC (PNW footprint: Seattle/Puget Sound market; Oregon & SW Washington market based in Portland; Spokane/Boise market covering eastern WA and Idaho)
- **Foundation / programs:**
  - National: Bank of America Charitable Foundation, which runs local-market RFP grants, Neighborhood Builders, and employee Matching Gifts.
  - PNW: grants are made market by market (Seattle; Oregon & SW Washington; Spokane/Boise) through local market presidents and regional foundation officers.
  - Sources: https://about.bankofamerica.com/en/making-an-impact/grant-funding-for-nonprofits-sponsorship-programs ; https://about.bankofamerica.com/en/local/seattle ; https://about.bankofamerica.com/en/local/portland-southwestwashington ; https://about.bankofamerica.com/en/local/spokane-boise
- **Focus areas:**
  - The foundation works on economic mobility.
  - The 2026 RFPs were "Basic Needs & Income Creation" and "Stable Housing & Empowering Communities".
  - Neighborhood Builders 2026 funds affordable housing / homeownership, plus workforce development, entrepreneurship and financial resilience.
  - Sources: https://researchfunding.duke.edu/bank-america-charitable-foundation-funding-opportunities ; https://grantedai.com/blog/bank-of-america-neighborhood-builders-2026-100k-400k-july-1-deadline-501c3-leadership-development-strategy
- **Typical grant size:**
  - Neighborhood Builders pays $200,000 over two years plus leadership training (Portland-market description). 2026 national materials cite $100K–$400K. Source: https://www.habitatportlandregion.org/habitat-for-humanity-named-bank-of-america-neighborhood-builders-awardee/ (via search snippet) ; https://collaborateandelevate.substack.com/p/bank-of-americas-2026-neighborhood
  - RFP grant sizes: unverified.
  - Past ACS grant: $50,000 in 2015 (Dallas, below).
- **Geographic limits:**
  - Applicants must be 501(c)(3) public charities that are based in, and serve, Bank of America markets.
  - No fiscal sponsors.
  - Source: https://about.bankofamerica.com/en/making-an-impact/eligibility-criteria
- **How to apply:**
  - Online application during posted RFP windows only. "Unsolicited applications are not accepted outside of posted dates"; deadline is 11:59 p.m. on the closing day.
  - Neighborhood Builders uses an open application during its window, with selection by the local market.
  - Sources: https://about.bankofamerica.com/en/making-an-impact/charitable-foundation-grant-faq ; https://about.bankofamerica.com/en/making-an-impact/neighborhood-builders-eligibility-criteria
- **Open RFPs / deadlines:**
  - 2026 windows (all closed): Basic Needs & Income Creation, Feb 2–Mar 2, 2026; Stable Housing & Empowering Communities, May 18–Jun 29, 2026; Neighborhood Builders, Jun 1–Jul 1, 2026.
  - 2027 dates are not yet published (unverified). A similar Feb/May pattern is likely but is an assumption.
  - Sources: https://researchfunding.duke.edu/bank-america-charitable-foundation-funding-opportunities ; https://grantedai.com/grants/neighborhood-builders-bank-of-america-foundation-2d880ecf
- **Local leadership (public roles):**
  - Seattle: Jim Morehead, President, Bank of America Seattle (named Dec 2022; still listed on the Jan 1, 2025 fact sheet). Source: https://newsroom.bankofamerica.com/content/newsroom/press-releases/2022/12/bofa-names-jim-morehead-president-of-seattle.html ; https://about.bankofamerica.com/content/dam/about/report-center/fact-sheets/united-states/washington/seattle_localfactsheet.pdf
  - Oregon & SW Washington: Marc Compton, named President in May 2026. Source: https://newsroom.bankofamerica.com/content/newsroom/press-releases/2026/05/bank-of-america-names-marc-compton-as-oregon-and-southwest-washi.html
  - Spokane/Boise: Lauren Murray, named President in Oct 2024 (succeeding Kurt Walsdorf). Source: https://newsroom.bankofamerica.com/content/newsroom/press-releases/2024/10/bofa-names-lauren-murray-president-of-spokane-boise.html
- **Local track record:**
  - 41 Puget Sound nonprofits have been named Neighborhood Builders since 2004. Seattle's 2025 awardees included The Coffee Oasis and HomeSight. Source: https://www.homesightwa.org/homesight-receives-bank-of-americas-neighborhood-builders-award/ ; Seattle fact sheet above.
  - 40 Portland-area Neighborhood Builders, with about $8M delivered. Source: https://habitatportlandregion.org/habitat-for-humanity-named-bank-of-america-neighborhood-builders-awardee/
- **Past cancer / ACS ties:**
  - ACS partner page: more than 50 years of support through grants, sponsorships, matching gifts and volunteering. Source: https://www.cancer.org/about-us/our-partners/bank-of-america.html
  - A $50,000 BofA Charitable Foundation grant in 2015 funded ACS transportation to treatment in Dallas, TX, under the foundation's "basic needs" priority. This is a Road to Recovery-type precedent. Same source.
  - Employee Matching Gifts have raised "millions of dollars" for ACS over five years. Same source.
  - BofA Community Volunteer Leaders have led regional Relay For Life and Making Strides engagement and served on ACS boards. Same source.
  - Recurring $25,000 "Diamond" sponsor of the ACS Financial Services Cares Gala (NYC). Source: https://fsc.acsgala.org/sponsors/
  - PNW-specific ACS sponsorships (e.g., Portland Hope Gala): unverified. The sponsor page could not be opened: https://portland.acsgala.org/sponsors/
- **Employee giving / matching:**
  - Matching is 1:1 from $25 up to $5,000 per employee per calendar year. Match requests are due Mar 31 of the following year.
  - Volunteer grants: $250 at 50 hours and $500 at 100 hours.
  - Annual giving-campaign gifts are not match-eligible.
  - Sources: https://doublethedonation.com/matching-gifts/bank-of-america ; https://about.bankofamerica.com/en/making-an-impact/employees-in-action
- **Public grants / contact page:** https://about.bankofamerica.com/en/making-an-impact/grant-funding-for-nonprofits-sponsorship-programs (sponsorships: https://www.bankofamericasponsorships.com/)
- **Suggested Tier:** 2
- **ACS angle:** The 2015 Dallas ride grant is a direct precedent for pitching Road to Recovery as "basic needs / access to care" in the 2027 Basic Needs RFP (likely February 2027) through the Seattle, Portland and Spokane/Boise markets. In the meantime, grow employee matching and volunteer-leader engagement around Relay For Life and Making Strides.

## KeyBank / KeyBank Foundation

- **HQ:** Cleveland, OH (PNW footprint: branches across WA, OR and ID; separate markets for Washington State, Oregon & SW Washington, and Idaho)
- **Foundation / programs:**
  - National: KeyBank Foundation Community Grants (invitation only, after an Expression of Interest), plus employee and community matching gift programs.
  - PNW: grants are routed through local market representatives.
  - Visible local examples:
    - $200,000 Bicentennial CDFI grant to Business Impact NW (Seattle, Aug 6, 2025).
    - $450,000 grant to FareStart (Seattle).
    - $20,000 grant to a Nampa, ID youth program.
    - KeyBank Community Plaza / official retail bank partnership with Providence Park and the Portland Thorns.
  - Sources: https://www.seattlechamber.com/news/2025/08/11/member-news/keybank-celebrates-its-bicentennial-with-a-200000-foundation-grant-to-business-impact-northwest-in-washington/ ; https://www.accessnewswire.com/newsroom/en/banking-and-financial-services/farestart-receives-450000-grant-from-keybank-foundation-984063 ; https://www.thorns.com/news/providence-park-thorns-fc-to-unveil-keybank-community-plaza-in-new-multiyear-partnership
- **Focus areas:**
  - Three priorities: Neighbors (safe, healthy, affordable, inclusive communities; housing and small business), Education, and Workforce.
  - Health and cancer are not core priorities.
  - Source: https://www.key.com/about/corporate-responsibility/keybank-foundation.html ; https://www.instrumentl.com/grants/keybank-foundation-grants
- **Typical grant size:**
  - Community Grants run $10,000–$25,000, averaging about $15,000. Applicants need an operating budget of at least $100K.
  - Larger strategic grants exist (e.g., $200K and $450K above).
  - Source: https://grantedai.com/grants/community-grants-keybank-keybank-24d2f7b4
- **Geographic limits:** KeyBank states only: AK, CO, CT, ID, IN, ME, MA, MI, NY, OH, OR, PA, UT, VT, WA. Source: https://www.insidephilanthropy.com/find-a-grant/grants-k/keybank-foundation
- **How to apply:**
  - Submit an online Expression of Interest (in use since 2025). A local representative responds within 90 days.
  - A full Community Grant request is by invitation only.
  - A new grantee process started July 1, 2025.
  - Source: https://www.key.com/about/corporate-responsibility/apply-for-a-grant.html
- **Open RFPs / deadlines:** EOIs are rolling, accepted January through October only. The 2026 window closes at the end of October 2026 (exact date unverified) and reopens in January 2027. Source: https://grantedai.com/grants/community-grants-keybank-keybank-24d2f7b4
- **Local leadership (public roles):**
  - Washington State: Brian Marlow, Market President (Oct 2024). Source: https://www.seattlechamber.com/news/2024/10/15/member-news/keybank-names-brian-marlow-market-president-for-washington-state/
  - Oregon & SW Washington: Josh Lyons, Market President. Source: https://www.linkedin.com/in/josh-lyons-0087b99/ (title per LinkedIn; confirm).
  - Idaho: Scott Schlange, Market President. Source: https://idahobusinessreview.com/2026/03/27/scott-schlange-leads-keybank-growth-idaho-market/
- **Past cancer / ACS ties:**
  - Historic $1 million KeyBank Foundation grant to Susan G. Komen for breast cancer education and outreach, extended to Bellingham, Bellevue, Tacoma and Portland (year not confirmed; older release). Source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/1-million-grant-from-keybank-foundation-will-fund-breast-cancer-education-and-support-programs-through-susan-g-komen-for-the-cure-119020804.html
  - Employee team "KeyBank Fab 4" at Relay For Life of Shoreline–North Seattle (2021). Source: https://secure.acsevents.org/site/TR/RelayForLife/RFLCY21WER?team_id=2571168&pg=team&fr_id=100648
  - No corporate ACS sponsorship found (unverified).
- **Employee giving / matching:**
  - Matching is 1:1 from $25 up to $2,000 per employee per year. An extra $2,000 applies if the employee sits on the nonprofit's board or capital-campaign committee.
  - Source: https://www.key.com/about/benefits/well-being/community-gift-programs.html
- **Public grants / contact page:** https://www.key.com/about/corporate-responsibility/apply-for-a-grant.html
- **Suggested Tier:** 3
- **ACS angle:** The Komen precedent shows KeyBank has funded cancer outreach before. An EOI before the end of October could frame patient navigation or Road to Recovery as keeping patients employed and in the workforce, under the "Workforce" or "healthy communities" priority. Realistically, the stronger near-term route is KeyBank employee Relay teams and matching gifts.

## Wells Fargo / Wells Fargo Foundation

- **HQ:** San Francisco, CA. This comes from background knowledge and is unverified; the company has moved major functions elsewhere. (PNW footprint: large branch networks in WA, OR and ID; Oregon & SW Washington region.)
- **Foundation / programs:**
  - National: Wells Fargo Foundation (strategic grants); Wells Fargo Community Giving (local grants); Housing Affordability Breakthrough Challenge (with Enterprise; five $2M grants in the 2026 cycle); a sponsorship portal; Community Care Grants for employees.
  - PNW: local grants go through regional Community Giving. Example: a $200,000 grant to the Idaho FFA Foundation (April 2026).
  - Sources: https://www.wellsfargo.com/about/responsibility-and-impact/community-giving/ ; https://capitalpress.com/2026/04/09/idaho-ffa-foundation-receives-ag-experience-grant-from-wells-fargo/ ; https://sponsorships.wf.com/
- **Focus areas:**
  - Financial opportunity (financial health), housing access and affordability, and small business growth.
  - Limited local support for disaster relief and the arts.
  - Source: https://www.wellsfargo.com/about/responsibility-and-impact/community-giving/grant-process/ ; https://www.theshareway.com/grant/wells-fargo-00211-wells-fargo-community-giving
- **Typical grant size:** Unverified for local grants. Known examples: $200K (Idaho FFA) and $2M (Breakthrough Challenge).
- **Geographic limits:** Wells Fargo communities only; applicants must be 501(c)(3)s, government, tribal or school entities. Source: grant-process page above.
- **How to apply:**
  - Invitation only. The foundation is "not currently accepting unsolicited grant interest forms"; invited orgs apply with an invitation code.
  - Sponsorships go through the online portal at https://sponsorships.wf.com/ (lead time unverified).
  - Source: https://www.wellsfargo.com/about/responsibility-and-impact/community-giving/grant-process/
- **Open RFPs / deadlines:** None open to uninvited applicants. The Community Giving cycle reportedly runs February to October (third-party listing; unverified).
- **Local leadership (public roles):** Tracy Curtis was Oregon & SW Washington Region President per 2023 sources. Whether she still holds the role in 2026 is unverified. Source: https://oregonbusiness.com/18675-downtime-with-tracy-curtis-region-president-of-wells-fargo/
- **Past cancer / ACS ties (strong):**
  - Five-time ACS "Excellence in Philanthropy" award recipient, given to companies that contribute $1M+ to ACS in total across corporate gifts, cause marketing, sponsorship, employee giving and events.
  - Employees give over $500,000 a year to ACS through payroll giving.
  - More than $4.3M raised for Relay For Life over several years; in 2015 alone, 102 teams and 672 participants raised over $360K.
  - These figures come from the ACS partner page. Its date is unknown and it may be several years old, so current status is unverified.
  - Source: https://www.cancer.org/about-us/our-partners/wells-fargo.html
  - PNW-specific ACS sponsorships: unverified.
- **Employee giving / matching:**
  - Matching gifts 1:1, minimum $25, maximum $2,000 (third-party figures).
  - Community Care Grants reward employee giving and volunteering all year, directed to charities of the employee's choice.
  - Sources: https://doublethedonation.com/matching-gifts/wells-fargo-company ; https://www.glassdoor.com/Benefits/Wells-Fargo-Charitable-Gift-Matching-US-BNFT21_E8876_N1.htm
- **Public grants / contact page:** https://www.wellsfargo.com/about/responsibility-and-impact/community-giving/grant-process/ and https://sponsorships.wf.com/
- **Suggested Tier:** 2
- **ACS angle:** Wells Fargo has a proven $1M+ national ACS relationship driven mostly by employees (payroll giving, Relay teams). The PNW play is to re-activate Wells Fargo Relay and Making Strides teams and workplace giving in Portland, Seattle and Boise. A grant invitation is unlikely because the priorities are financial health, housing and small business.
