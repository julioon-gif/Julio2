## Amazon
- **HQ:** Seattle, WA (HQ1, Puget Sound)
- **Giving programs:**
  - Amazon community impact, Puget Sound/HQ1: housing, food security, education, disaster relief. https://www.aboutamazon.com/impact/community ; https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/community/amazon-seattle-bellevue-puget-sound-news
  - Amazon Housing Equity Fund: more than 10,000 affordable homes created or preserved in the region; more than $900M in local housing investment. https://www.amazonhousingequity.com/community-impact/puget-sound-region ; https://businessdailynetwork.com/stories/675649562-amazon-outlines-puget-sound-investments-in-housing-hunger-relief-and-community-support
  - AWS Imagine Grant: national program for 501(c) nonprofits that use technology. Three categories: Pathfinder, Go Further Faster, Momentum to Modernize. https://aws.amazon.com/government-education/nonprofits/aws-imagine-grant-program/
  - AWS Health Equity Initiative: $60M in cloud credits in total, up to $250K in credits per award. **Not accepting applications as of Aug 2026.** https://aws.amazon.com/government-education/nonprofits/global-social-impact/health-equity/ ; https://www.fiercehealthcare.com/finance/aws-commits-additional-20m-health-equity-initiative
  - Candid profile: Amazon.com Contributions Program, which funds literacy, arts and disaster relief, "primarily national organizations." https://fconline.foundationcenter.org/fdo-grantmaker-profile/?collection=grantmakers&activity=result&key=AMAZ007
- **Focus areas:** affordable housing, hunger relief, youth and education (computer science), disaster relief, literary arts; AWS: technology for nonprofits, formerly including health equity.
- **Typical grant size:**
  - Local example: $80,000 to Still Waters (Tukwila food program). https://grantedai.com/grants/puget-sound-amazon-0dbc3685
  - Local example: $1M to Rainier Valley Food Bank's capital campaign. https://businessdailynetwork.com/stories/675649562-amazon-outlines-puget-sound-investments-in-housing-hunger-relief-and-community-support
  - AWS Imagine award amounts: unverified (the guidelines page was blocked).
- **Geographic limits:** HQ1 giving is Puget Sound (Seattle, Bellevue, South King County). AWS Imagine is open to US nonprofits nationwide.
- **How to apply:** HQ1 community grants have no public open application (invitation or relationship-based; unverified). AWS Imagine is an open competitive application.
- **Open RFPs and deadlines:** AWS Imagine 2026 Round One closed **June 5, 2026**. https://opportunitydesk.org/2026/04/13/aws-imagine-grant-2026/ ; https://apply.younoodle.com/showcase/competition/2026_aws_imagine_grant. The 2027 cycle is not yet announced (a 2027 opening around spring is likely based on past pattern, but unverified). Health Equity Initiative: closed.
- **Past cancer or ACS giving:**
  - No Amazon corporate gift to ACS found.
  - AWS staff (with partner PTP) volunteered serving dinner at the ACS Hope Lodge in Boston. https://ptp.cloud/hope-lodge-boston-2024/
  - AWS Health Equity Initiative funded Navigating Cancer's Health Tracker (cancer patient-reported outcomes). https://www.navigatingcare.com/navigating-cancer-selected-for-amazon-web-services-aws-health-equity-initiative/
  - Related but not corporate: the Bezos family gave $710.5M to Fred Hutch in 2022. https://www.fredhutch.org/en/news/releases/2022/10/fred-hutchinson-cancer-center-receives--710-5-million-gift-to-ac.html
- **Employee giving and matching:** Conflicting. Double the Donation lists a 1:1 match up to $5,000/yr; a 2023 employee thread says there is no matching program. **Unverified.** https://doublethedonation.com/matching-gifts/amazon ; https://www.teamblind.com/post/no-matching-gift-program-at-amazon-zcrjhvbu. AmazonSmile ended Feb 20, 2023. https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/company-news/amazon-closing-amazonsmile-to-focus-its-philanthropic-giving-to-programs-with-greater-impact
- **Public grants or contact page:** https://aws.amazon.com/government-education/nonprofits/aws-imagine-grant-program/ ; https://www.aboutamazon.com/impact/community
- **Suggested Tier:** 3. It becomes Tier 2 if ACS pursues a tech-modernization proposal through AWS Imagine 2027.
