## Albertsons Companies / Albertsons Companies Foundation (incl. Safeway WA/OR)
- **HQ:** Boise, ID. The foundation is registered in Pleasanton, CA (EIN 91-2144510, formerly The Safeway Foundation). Source: https://www.causeiq.com/organizations/the-albertsons-companies-foundation,912144510/
- **Giving programs:** Albertsons Companies Foundation, with regional grant programs by division. Relevant PNW divisions: **Seattle division** (WA, ID, AK), **Portland division** (Portland, OR and SW Washington) and **Intermountain division** (ID, MT, UT, ND and parts of NV/WY). Also in-store checkout ("round up") campaigns. Sources: http://albertsonsmarketfoundation.org/get-funded/grant-funding-guidelines-seattle-wa-id-ak/ ; http://safewayfoundation.org/get-funded/grant-funding-guidelines-portland-southwest-washington/ ; http://acmemarketsfoundation.org/get-funded/grant-funding-guidelines-intermountain/
- **Focus areas:** Health and Human Services (including cancer research and treatment), hunger (Nourishing Neighbors), youth and education, and veterans. The Portland and Intermountain guidelines explicitly name cancer: research, increasing enrollment in cancer trials, access to cancer preventive testing, and access to alternative/complementary cancer treatment. Sources: https://www.insidephilanthropy.com/find-a-grant/grants-a/albertsons-companies-foundation ; http://safewayfoundation.org/get-funded/grant-funding-guidelines-portland-southwest-washington/ ; https://www.theshareway.com/grant/albertsons-companies-19991-albertsons-foundation-intermountain
- **Typical grant size:** A first-time grantee usually gets $1,000–$5,000 outside a special RFP (Portland guidelines). The Seattle region cites $2,500–$10,000. Source: search snippets of the guideline pages above, plus https://grantedai.com/grants/albertsons-companies-foundation-community-grants-albertsons-companies-foundation-a938d245 (the size ranges come from snippets, so treat them as partly verified).
- **Geographic limits:** 501(c)(3)s serving communities with Albertsons/Safeway stores, reviewed by the division team.
- **How to apply:** Online application, which is routed to the right division team. Review timing differs by division: Seattle reviews twice a year (January and July); Portland and Intermountain review monthly (a decision takes about 2 to 6 weeks). Special RFPs are **by invitation only**. Some regions (CA, HI, Reno) require an employee sponsor; the PNW does not appear to. Sources: http://albertsonsmarketfoundation.org/get-funded/grant-application-process-seattle/ ; http://safewayfoundation.org/get-funded/faqs-portland/ ; http://safewayfoundation.org/get-funded/
- **Open RFPs/deadlines:** No exact dates found. The next Seattle-division review is expected in January 2027 (unverified).
- **Past cancer/ACS giving:**
  - Safeway ran an annual national breast cancer checkout campaign that raised more than $25M over 6 years, with $8.3M in 2006. Beneficiaries included Fred Hutch. Source: https://www.fredhutch.org/en/news/center-news/2007/07/mobile-mammography.html ; https://progressivegrocer.com/safeway-raises-145-million-breast-cancer
  - Safeway's Seattle division gave $800K to Fred Hutch and UW Medicine, plus $200K for the SCCA mobile mammography van operations (2007). Source: https://www.fredhutch.org/en/news/center-news/2007/07/mobile-mammography.html
  - The Safeway Foundation partnered with Stand Up To Cancer on breast cancer campaigns (with Wanda Sykes). Source: http://albertsonscompaniesfoundation.org/wanda-sykes-teams-stand-cancer-safeway-foundation-launch-new-campaign-raise-funds-awareness-battle-breast-cancer/
  - The Kids 4 Hope checkout campaign with City of Hope (Southern California) raised more than $1M. Source: https://www.biospace.com/albertsons-vons-and-pavilions-stores-raise-over-1-million-for-city-of-hope-s-research-and-treatment-for-childhood-cancers
  - The Westerlay Orchids breast cancer cause promotion ran in Albertsons stores in October 2025. Source: https://www.independent.com/2025/11/06/westerlay-orchids-and-albertsons-kroger-and-sobeys-team-up-to-raise-30450-for-breast-cancer-research-centers/
  - No direct ACS partnership was found (unverified/none found).
- **Employee giving/matching:** A matching gift program is listed on Double the Donation, but its terms could not be confirmed (unverified). Source: https://doublethedonation.com/matching-gifts/albertsons-companies
- **Grants/contact page:** http://safewayfoundation.org/get-funded/ (Get Funded, all divisions) ; https://www.albertsonscompanies.com/about-aci/our-impact/Albertsons-Foundation/default.aspx
- **Suggested Tier:** **1**
- **ACS angle:** Cancer research, preventive testing and patient support are named priorities in the Portland and Intermountain guidelines, so apply there for Road to Recovery, screening navigation or research. Separately, pitch Safeway's Seattle and Portland divisions and Albertsons Intermountain on an October checkout campaign that revives Safeway's legacy breast cancer campaign, benefiting ACS/Making Strides.

## Blue Cross of Idaho / Blue Cross of Idaho Foundation for Health
- **HQ:** Meridian, ID. The foundation is based in Boise/Meridian (EIN 26-0024334). Source: https://www.zeffy.com/foundations/260024334 ; https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/organizations/260024334
- **Giving programs:**
  - (a) Blue Cross of Idaho **corporate charitable contributions and sponsorships**. Source: https://corporate.bcidaho.com/company-information/charitable-contributions.page
  - (b) The **Blue Cross of Idaho Foundation for Health** (private foundation, est. 2001), which runs these programs:
    - Employee Community Fund (ECF)
    - Community Health Academy
    - The Community Project (up to $85K for outdoor spaces, only for cities that attended the Academy)
    - Community Transformation Grant
    - Health Professions CTE grants (up to $50K; the 2026 deadline was 2026-03-02 and has passed)
    - Community Health Champion award ($5K)

    Sources: https://www.bcidahofoundation.org/ ; https://www.bcidahofoundation.org/community-project/ ; https://www.bcidahofoundation.org/funding-opportunity-health-professions-programs/ ; https://www.bcidahofoundation.org/cambridge-2026-community-health-champion/ ; https://www.bcidahofoundation.org/community-transformation-grant/
- **Focus areas:** Foundation: community health, built environment and active living, youth wellness, early education, healthcare workforce, behavioral and mental health (ECF), and supportive housing. ECF gives priority to seniors, youth, and underserved or underrepresented groups. No cancer-specific program found. Sources: https://philanthropynw.org/resources/guest/stories/blue-cross-idaho-foundation-healths-transformational-approach-meets ; https://www.bcidahofoundation.org/contact-us/ecf-grants/
- **Typical grant size:** ECF gives up to $5,000 and is not renewable in consecutive years. Corporate contributions have no stated dollar limit. Sources: https://www.bcidahofoundation.org/contact-us/ecf-grants/ ; https://corporate.bcidaho.com/company-information/charitable-contributions.page
- **Geographic limits:** Idaho only, and for the corporate program only in markets where the company does business in Idaho.
- **How to apply:** Corporate: submit the application form to the company's published contributions inbox (see the page). A committee meets monthly, and applicants hear back within about 60 days. ECF: an open annual cycle scored by an employee committee. The 2025 cycle ran May 12 to June 20, 2025. The 2026 dates were not confirmed, and a third-party site lists June 16, 2027 as the next deadline (unverified). Sources: https://corporate.bcidaho.com/company-information/charitable-contributions.page ; https://www.bcidahofoundation.org/contact-us/ecf-grants/
- **Open RFPs/deadlines:** None in the next 90 days confirmed. The ECF cycle likely opens around May 2027 (unverified).
- **Past cancer/ACS giving:** None found. Blue Cross of Idaho's member health education cites ACS screening guidelines and promotes breast and colorectal screening and mobile mammography. Sources: https://www.bcidaho.com/health-education/colorectal-cancer-screening ; https://www.bcidaho.com/health-education/breast-cancer-screening
- **Employee giving/matching:** An annual employee giving campaign (Employee Giving Fund) feeds the ECF. Corporate matching was not found (unverified). Source: https://www.bcidahofoundation.org/employee-giving-fund/ ; https://www.bcidaho.com/news-releases/2019-0415-Blue-Cross-of-Idaho-Employee-Giving-Fund
- **Grants/contact page:** https://corporate.bcidaho.com/company-information/charitable-contributions.page ; https://www.bcidahofoundation.org/contact-us/ecf-grants/
- **Suggested Tier:** **2**
- **ACS angle:** As an insurer, Blue Cross of Idaho already promotes breast and colorectal screening. Pitch a corporate sponsorship or contribution tied to rural Idaho colorectal and lung screening navigation, HPV vaccination, or Road to Recovery rides for rural members. The ECF could fund a small ($5K) rural patient-support project.

## Boise Cascade
- **HQ:** Boise, ID.
- **Giving program:** Corporate community contributions, with no separate foundation found. The company describes giving "time, talent, and treasures": group volunteering, table sponsorships at fundraisers, and associates serving on nonprofit boards. Sources: https://www.bc.com/community/ ; https://www.bc.com/community-contributions/
- **Focus areas:** Organizations that "align with its values and provide measurable and meaningful impact to help people and communities thrive". Specific categories were not confirmed (unverified).
- **Typical grant size:** Unverified / not found.
- **Geographic limits:** Communities around its 60+ locations. Each location has its own autonomy and budget. PNW facility list not confirmed (unverified).
- **How to apply:** Idaho HQ requests are accepted year-round through the community contributions page and decided quarterly. Outside Idaho, contact the nearest facility using the company location map. Source: https://www.bc.com/community-contributions/
- **Open RFPs/deadlines:** None. Rolling, with quarterly decisions.
- **Past cancer/ACS giving:** Boise Cascade has a team fundraising page for Leukemia & Lymphoma Society Light The Night (Twin Cities 2024), which is employee-driven cancer fundraising but not ACS. It has sponsored the Idaho Women's Charitable Foundation. No ACS gifts found. Sources: https://pages.lls.org/ltn/mn/TwinCiti24/BoiseCascade ; https://www.facebook.com/idahowomenscharitablefoundation/posts/boise-cascade-does-so-much-for-our-community-thank-you-so-much-for-sponsoring-th/869000625252632/
- **Employee giving/matching:** Boise Cascade matches employee charitable contributions (it did so for southern Oregon wildfire recovery). Program-wide terms are unverified. Source: https://www.bc.com/matching-wildfire-donations/ ; https://www.bc.com/community/
- **Grants/contact page:** https://www.bc.com/community-contributions/
- **Suggested Tier:** **3**
- **ACS angle:** The company already runs employee team fundraising for LLS. Pitch Boise HQ and PNW mill teams on Relay For Life or Making Strides teams and a gala table sponsorship, framed around wellness and cancer screening for its largely rural manufacturing workforce.
