## Weyerhaeuser

- **HQ:** Seattle, WA. VERIFIED by the SEC EDGAR business address, 220 Occidental Avenue South, Seattle, WA 98104: https://data.sec.gov/submissions/CIK0000106535.json
- **Foundation or program:** Weyerhaeuser Giving Fund, the company's main grantmaking vehicle. Local employee advisory committees direct the grants. VERIFIED: https://www.weyerhaeuser.com/company/values/citizenship/giving-fund/ and https://www.weyerhaeuser.com/company/values/citizenship/
- **Focus areas:** Seven focus areas: affordable housing, education and youth development, environmental stewardship, **human services**, civic and cultural growth, workforce development, and inclusion. Health and cancer are **not** named as focus areas. VERIFIED (Giving Fund page).
  - Exclusions that matter for ACS: sponsorships and tickets or tables at fundraising benefits, sports or athletic events, overhead and indirect costs, endowments and memorials, capital campaigns, and multiyear requests. VERIFIED (Giving Fund page). In practice this rules out ACS galas and Relay sponsorships. A request has to be program-specific.
  - National grants (invitation only) cover only working-forest sustainability. VERIFIED.
- **Typical grant size:** The minimum is $1,000. Grants "vary in scale in relationship to our presence in the community". VERIFIED (Giving Fund FAQ). Total giving in 2025 was $5.9M across grants, partnerships, matching gifts, research support and in-kind. VERIFIED: https://www.weyerhaeuser.com/company/values/citizenship/
- **Geographic limits:** Only communities with operations or company-owned or managed forestland in the US and Canada. Applicants must be US 501(c)(3)s (or Canadian equivalents). VERIFIED.
  - **WA:** Corporate HQ in the Seattle and Tacoma areas; facility communities Aberdeen, Centralia/Chehalis, Longview, Olympia, Pe Ell, Raymond, Rainier; land-base counties Clallam, Clark, Cowlitz, Grays Harbor, King, Lewis, Pacific, Pierce, Skamania, Snohomish, Thurston, Wahkiakum. VERIFIED: https://www.weyerhaeuser.com/company/values/citizenship/giving-fund/where-we-give-washington/
  - **OR:** facility communities Cottage Grove, Eugene, Springfield, Albany, Lebanon, Sweet Home; land-base counties Benton, Clackamas, Clatsop, Columbia, Coos, Douglas, Lane, Lincoln, Linn, Marion, Multnomah, Polk, Tillamook, Washington, Yamhill. VERIFIED: https://www.weyerhaeuser.com/company/values/citizenship/giving-fund/where-we-give-oregon/
  - **ID:** Boise. VERIFIED (main Giving Fund page; Idaho has no separate county page).
- **How to apply:** Online portal only; paper is not accepted. The page recommends making a local connection with the employee advisory committee first. Email the Giving Fund to get a local committee contact. You will need a W-9, organization and project details, and financials. Decisions often come in winter, after the September deadline. VERIFIED (Giving Fund page). The portal link is not exposed in the page HTML, so ask givingfund@weyerhaeuser.com for it.
- **Open RFPs and deadlines (next 12 months):** The 2026 deadline (Sept 30, 2026) has passed. VERIFIED. The 2027 cycle opening date and deadline are **not published**. A September 30 deadline is plausible for 2027 by pattern, but it is not confirmed and must not be used as a date.
- **Past cancer or ACS ties:**
  - A Mississippi news story reports a $10,000 Weyerhaeuser grant to ACS from local plant grant money, plus a company golf tournament that became a Relay For Life fundraiser. UNVERIFIED (news, undated): https://neshobademocrat.com/stories/weyerhaeuser-gives-10000-grant-to-help-battle-cancer,48735
  - Weyerhaeuser was listed in ACS's 2006 Relay For Life National Corporate Team Program. UNVERIFIED (2006 press release): https://www.biospace.com/american-cancer-society-announces-2006-relay-for-life-r-national-corporate-team-program

  - No PNW ACS tie was found.
- **Employee giving and matching:**
  - TREE-mendous Matching Gifts matches eligible employee donations **and volunteer time** up to $1,500 per person per year. In 2025: 24,000+ volunteer hours, 788 volunteer activities, and $321,351 in volunteer rewards. VERIFIED: https://www.weyerhaeuser.com/company/values/citizenship/
  - Volunteer of the Year Award: a $5,000 grant to the honoree's organization. VERIFIED (same page).

- **Grants or contact page:** https://www.weyerhaeuser.com/company/values/citizenship/giving-fund/. Public inbox: givingfund@weyerhaeuser.com. VERIFIED.
- **Suggested Tier:** 2. There is a craftable "human services" tie in timber counties. Health is not a stated focus, and events and sponsorships are excluded.

Frame it as "human services" access for the company's own communities, and line up a local advisory-committee contact before the 2027 cycle. Separately, recruit Weyerhaeuser employee volunteers so their hours earn TREE-mendous volunteer rewards for ACS.

- **HQ:** Seattle, WA. - **Past cancer or ACS ties:**

  - On Deck for a Cause (launched 2006) raised more than $5M–$6M for cancer charities, with the **American Cancer Society** among the beneficiaries in the mid-to-late 2010s. UNVERIFIED (trade press and press-release snippets): https://www.seatrade-cruise.com/ship-operations/holland-america-line-reaches-6m-donated-to-cancer-support and https://app.3blmedia.com/news/holland-america-line-reaches-milestone-5-million-donated-cancer-support-through-deck

It also sponsored the ACS Washington Invitational Golf Tournament and the Seattle Hope Gala (2016). UNVERIFIED (Carnival and 3BL press-release snippets; the carnivalcorp.com release pages now redirect to a list): https://www.carnivalcorp.com/news-releases/news-release-details/holland-america-line-hosts-american-cancer-societys-toast-life
  - The cancer focus was later dropped for Direct Relief and Alaska Geographic, and in March 2026 for Conservation International. VERIFIED (2026 release).
- **Employee giving and matching:** Seattle employees ran United Way campaigns, Northwest Harvest volunteering and holiday Adopt-a-Family drives. UNVERIFIED (2015–2018 press snippets): https://www.carnivalcorp.com/news-releases/news-release-details/holland-america-line-wraps-successful-year-seattle-giving. No current matching-gift terms were found.
