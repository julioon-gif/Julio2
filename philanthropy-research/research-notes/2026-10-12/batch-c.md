## Walmart (Walmart Spark Good / Walmart Foundation)

- **HQ:** Bentonville, AR (PNW: Walmart and Sam's Club stores and distribution centers across WA/OR/ID). HQ is common knowledge; the store footprint was not counted today. The Portland, OR store #5440 grant example is VERIFIED on walmart.org.
- **Foundation / program:**
  - **Spark Good Local Grants** (store/club/DC-level cash grants). VERIFIED.
  - **Spark Good** umbrella: local grants, Round Up and Registry on Walmart.com, storefront reservation (space outside stores), and Associate Giving and volunteering. VERIFIED: https://walmart.org/what-we-do/strengthening-community/spark-good
  - **Walmart Foundation** national grants: "The majority of our funding is proactive and made to U.S. tax-exempt organizations ... independently identified by our staff". Concept notes are accepted only for one stated focus area (the name did not render in the page text) and are reviewed on a rolling basis with "no deadlines". VERIFIED: https://walmart.org/how-we-give/faqs
- **Focus areas (local grants):** Facility managers favor requests that address "a specific, demonstrated need in their community" where Walmart is "well positioned to contribute". Spotlighted categories include economic opportunity, sustainability, access to healthier food, disaster preparedness, and "building caring & connected communities". One spotlight grant was for care packages for people affected by Alzheimer's. VERIFIED (guidelines page). Health is not excluded.
- **Typical grant size:** $250 to $5,000 (VERIFIED). Spotlight examples range from $300 to $3,500.
- **Geographic limits:** Organizations "must serve the same service area that the facility they are requesting funding from serves". Eligible applicants are US 501(c)(3) public charities on the IRS Master File "conducting activities within the United States", plus government entities, schools and some faith-based community projects. VERIFIED. Non-US organizations use separate country programs (FAQ, VERIFIED).
- **How to apply:**
  1. Create a Spark Good account at Walmart.com/nonprofits.
  2. Get verified through Deed, Walmart's third-party verification partner.
  3. Apply from the Spark Good account to each store, club or DC whose service area ACS serves.

  Only authorized users may apply. An organization may have at most 25 pending or approved applications at once. Once funded, it cannot reapply to the same facility in the same fiscal year. Decisions are made before the next cycle opens, and banking must be set up within 21 days of notice. VERIFIED (guidelines page).
- **Open RFPs / deadlines (next 12 months):** **Cycle 3 closes Nov. 30 (2026)**. VERIFIED; see the top of this file for the year note. FY28 cycles are not yet posted.
- **Past cancer / ACS ties: strong.** ACS's own partner page says "Partner since 1960" and that "Walmart and its associates have contributed more than $100 million dollars to support the American Cancer Society". It also cites:
  - a Walmart Foundation health-disparities grant for community health advisors (breast and colon screening)
  - an HPV vaccination education commitment "in all U.S. pharmacy locations"
  - associate Relay For Life, Making Strides and Hope Lodge involvement
  - over $2 million from the Associate Giving Program

  Source: https://www.cancer.org/about-us/our-partners/walmart.html (this is ACS's page, not Walmart's, so the figures are ACS-reported). A 2011 ACS press release on a $2M Walmart Foundation screening grant is UNVERIFIED (snippet only): https://pressroom.cancer.org/2011-07-25-American-Cancer-Society-Receives-Walmart-Foundation-Grant-to-Increase-Access-to-Breast-and-Colon-Cancer-Screenings-in-Underserved-Communities
- **Employee giving / matching:** Associates give through the Spark Good Associate platform. Organizations can post volunteer opportunities there. Associate donations "are not automatically matched. Matching campaigns will be offered at least twice per fiscal year". VERIFIED (FAQ).
- **Grants / contact page:** https://walmart.org/how-we-give/program-guidelines/spark-good-local-grants-guidelines ; FAQ https://walmart.org/how-we-give/faqs ; general inbox listed in the FAQ: walmart.org@walmart.com (the FAQ gives it for Northwest Arkansas Giving status questions).
- **Suggested Tier:** **1.** ACS's own page records a national ACS partnership since 1960, and local grants are open now in WA/OR/ID.
- **ACS angle:** Before Nov 30, file several $1,000–$5,000 Spark Good requests to PNW stores and DCs near ACS service hubs. Good fits are Road to Recovery (gas cards and volunteer-driver support for rural patients) and screening-education events (HPV, colorectal), which build on Walmart's pharmacy HPV commitment. Also make sure ACS's Spark Good profile is Deed-verified so associate giving and the twice-yearly matching campaigns reach it.
