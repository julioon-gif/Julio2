## Best opportunities (summary)

1. **Aon (Tier 2).**
   - Aon already sponsors ACS elsewhere. It is a 2026 Silver Sponsor of the ACS gala in Green Bay, WI (VERIFIED on the ACS gala site).
   - Its 2025 giving priorities now explicitly include "Health and human services". Aon and NFP gave more than $17M to more than 2,500 charities in 2025 (VERIFIED).
   - Aon is about to acquire USI for $17B (VERIFIED, 8-K of 2026-08-31). That deal could bring new PNW offices and leaders into the relationship.
   - The angle is Aon's health and benefits consulting, with employer screening and workforce wellness tied to ACS's workplace programs.
2. **Symetra (Tier 2).**
   - It makes the largest discretionary local grants in this batch: one-time grants of $25K–$50K and multi-year commitments of $50K–$100K (UNVERIFIED).
   - Nine of its 19 grants in 2025 went to Puget Sound organizations (UNVERIFIED).
   - Its focus is economic mobility and housing, not health. The craftable tie is Hope Lodge (free lodging, which relieves the housing burden of treatment) or Road to Recovery (rides), framed as economic security for cancer patients.
   - A 2018 Symetra CEO Service Award winner directed her award to ACS (UNVERIFIED).
3. **Avanade (Tier 3).**
   - Employee programs are modest: a $250-per-year match and 8 paid volunteer hours (VERIFIED).
   - Employee cancer engagement already exists: well-being webinars on cancer (VERIFIED), and a Seattle Cycle for Survival team for Memorial Sloan Kettering (UNVERIFIED).
   - The new CEO, Chris Howarth, started on 2026-02-03 (VERIFIED).
4. **eXp / AGNT Inc. (Tier 3 for grants, Tier 2 for agent-network fundraising).**
   - No "eXp Cares" was found. The company charity, eXtend a Hand Fund, is an internal hardship fund for agents and staff (VERIFIED as an IRS filer, with eXp executives as trustees).
   - The parent company renamed itself AGNT, Inc. in June 2026 and changed CEO on 2026-10-08 (VERIFIED in SEC filings).
   - The realistic path is agent teams and local eXp offices for Relay, Making Strides and gala tables.
5. **KPFF (Tier 3).**
   - It has no foundation and no published giving process. It says it gives "financially to organizations in need at both the local and national level" (VERIFIED).
   - The new CEO, Scott Kuebler, started on 2025-06-06 (VERIFIED).
   - The path is employee and office-level sponsorship, for example a Seattle or Portland office gala table or a Making Strides team.
