# ACS PNW Corporate Prospects: Batch A (expansion research)

Research date: 2026-10-05. All five are current ACS corporate accounts, so these notes look for expansion openings.

Labels:
- **VERIFIED** means the fact was read on the company's or foundation's own page or official PDF, or in the company's own SEC filing.
- **UNVERIFIED** means it came from a search snippet, a grant directory, news, or a third-party aggregator. Confirm it before using it externally.

## Verified deadlines in next 90 days (2026-10-05 to 2027-01-03)

- **2026-12-04: Kroger Co. Foundation / Kroger Co. Zero Hunger | Zero Waste Foundation, quarterly grant deadline.** VERIFIED on https://www.communitygifts.com and in the Kroger Charitable Giving Guidelines PDF (Nov 2024 edition, linked from that portal): https://cdn.versaic.com/kroger/d1ec83a2-3272-4c33-b980-5bf54cda62fa. The 2026 deadlines listed are 04/03, 06/26, 09/18 and 12/04/2026. Apply through communitygifts.com. For ACS, the relevant focus area is "Health & nutrition". This is the route for a QFC or Fred Meyer request.
- No other verified deadlines fall in this window:
  - Numerica's donations and Charitable Fund are closed until "early 2027", and its sponsorships are reviewed each September.
  - F5's Community Impact Grants are employee-nominated, with no external deadline.
  - Fortive and Mechanics Bank publish no application process or dates.

## Best opportunities (summary)

1. **QFC / Fred Meyer / Kroger (Tier 1).** It has a verified open deadline (12/04/2026), "Health & nutrition" is an eligible focus area, the president of Kroger Health sits on the Kroger Co. Foundation board, and Kroger has an ACS relationship at the national level (unverified).
2. **F5 (Tier 2).** Grants are employee-driven: $5,000 Community Impact Grants every quarter, a 100% donation match, and a 10-day Giving Tuesday campaign that includes a $50 per-employee giving credit. Recruiting F5ers who volunteer with ACS is the lever. F5 has already funded a cancer nonprofit in Idaho.
3. **Fortive (Tier 2–3).** Its foundation pillars do not include health. The openings are its "community resilience" pillar, the Day of Caring, and its healthcare businesses (Fluke Biomedical, Advanced Healthcare Solutions).
4. **Numerica (Tier 3 for grants, Tier 2 for event sponsorship).** Its grants go to vulnerable youth and families. Event sponsorship is the realistic path, and the next review is September 2027.
5. **Mechanics Bank (Tier 3).** The HomeStreet Foundation appears to have dissolved (unverified). Giving is California-weighted and focused on low- and moderate-income communities, and no PNW application path was found.
