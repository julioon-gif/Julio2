## PACCAR

- **HQ:** Bellevue, WA.
- **Foundation/program:** PACCAR Foundation, a private foundation formed in 1951. VERIFIED (paccar.com).
- **Focus areas:** Grantees in communities with a significant PACCAR presence "usually include United Way, universities, hospitals and programs for the arts and economic education." Most other grants go to "capital campaigns involving acquisition or improvement of facilities used for social and health services, education and cultural affairs." Program funds, operating budgets and fundraising events are "seldom funded." VERIFIED.
- **Typical grant size:** Not stated on the funder's page. The FY2024 990-PF shows $6,853,712 in total grants paid (VERIFIED, IRS 990-PF via ProPublica). Inside Philanthropy says past grants ranged from $1,000 to $1 million (UNVERIFIED).
- **Geographic limits:** Grants usually go to "communities within the service area of a significant PACCAR presence, such as a factory or a major office." VERIFIED. The Bellevue/Puget Sound HQ area in WA qualifies. The page does not name OR or ID sites, so their eligibility is not confirmed. Applicants must be US 501(c)(3) organizations: the application asks for "evidence of 501(c)(3) tax-exempt status" and an IRS W-9. VERIFIED.
- **How to apply:** Online grant application, with no letter-of-inquiry step. Required items include history and goals, board list, annual report, audited financials, the specific amount requested, project and total budgets, a timeline and other funders. Rules: one request per organization per year, no event sponsorships or tickets, no phone solicitations, no automatic renewals. Decisions within six months. VERIFIED.
- **Open RFPs/deadlines (12 months):** None. Applications are rolling and the page lists no deadlines. VERIFIED.
- **Past cancer/ACS ties:**
  - PACCAR's 2025 Annual Report, filed with the SEC, names "Fred Hutchinson Cancer Research" among the nonprofits PACCAR supports. Its list of institutions supported by PACCAR Philanthropy includes "Fred Hutchison [sic] Cancer Research Center in Seattle, Washington." VERIFIED.
  - Inside Philanthropy lists the University of Texas MD Anderson Cancer Center, Seattle Children's and Swedish Medical Center Foundation among health grantees. UNVERIFIED.
  - No ACS grant could be confirmed. The 990-PF grant list sits in "Attachment A", a PDF schedule that is not in the IRS XML. ProPublica's PDF download returned a 403 security check, so the grantee list could not be read.
- **Employee giving/matching:** An Educational Matching Gifts Program form, hosted by Double the Donation, applies to educational institutions only. ACS would not qualify. UNVERIFIED (third-party copy of the form). The 2025 Annual Report says PACCAR employees "contribute their time and resources as volunteers and fundraisers." VERIFIED.
- **Grants/contact page:** https://www.paccar.com/about-us/paccar-foundation/ (online application link and a contact form on the page).
- **Suggested Tier:** 2. There is a documented cancer tie (Fred Hutch), but the funder favors capital projects in its HQ communities.
- **ACS angle:** Pitch a capital request in the Puget Sound area. Examples are facility improvements or a named-space gift at the Seattle Hope Lodge, or Road to Recovery vehicles if that counts as capital. This fits PACCAR's stated preference for "facilities used for social and health services" and its Fred Hutch history.

  - VERIFIED (company annual report on SEC EDGAR): https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/75362/000119312526113873/2025_annual_report_-_ars.pdf

## PacifiCorp (Pacific Power / Rocky Mountain Power)

- **HQ:** Portland, OR.
- **Foundation/program:** Pacific Power Foundation (PacifiCorp Foundation; ProPublica lists it as "Pacificorp Foundation," EIN 94-3089826). VERIFIED. Its mission is "to support the growth and vitality of local communities through charitable investments." It has awarded more than $13M over the past 5 years. VERIFIED.
- **Focus areas (four cycles, five categories):** VERIFIED.
  - Mar 15: Education/STEM.
  - Jun 15: Community Enhancement and Environmental Respect.
  - Sep 15: Arts/Culture.
  - Dec 15: Safety/Wellness. This covers addiction, disability resources, disaster relief, domestic violence, first responders, food insecurity, **healthcare accessibility**, homeless support, mental health, public safety, veterans, and **wellness and preventative care**.
  - Eligible applicants include "health and human services organizations... safety programs, and wellness programs."
- **Typical grant size:** "Grants generally are less than $10,000 with most between $2,000 and $5,000." Small capital grants go up to $5,000 in any cycle. VERIFIED. The FY2024 990-PF shows $2,794,533 in total grants paid. VERIFIED (IRS 990-PF via ProPublica).
- **Geographic limits:** Projects must benefit communities served by Pacific Power (Oregon, Central/Southeastern Washington and Northern California) or Rocky Mountain Power (Utah, Wyoming and Southeastern Idaho). Applicants list the cities and counties served, which "must be located within the utility service territory." Organizations outside the territory are ineligible. Applicants must be 501(c)(3) organizations that are not private foundations. VERIFIED. **This covers parts of WA, OR and ID, so it is not statewide.**
- **How to apply:** Create an account on the online grant portal; accounts are reviewed within 2 business days. Choose the cycle that matches the *organization*, not the project. A 150-word summary, the amount, the number of people served, the communities served, measurable objectives and other funders are required. Supporting documents are optional. Exclusions include endowments, operating deficits, capital campaigns, events and conferences, and marketing. VERIFIED.
- **Open RFPs/deadlines (12 months):** VERIFIED as recurring annual dates on the funder's page:
  - Dec 15, 2026: Safety/Wellness (**best fit**)
  - Mar 15, 2027: Education/STEM
  - Jun 15, 2027: Community Enhancement and Environmental Respect
  - Sep 15, 2027: Arts/Culture
- **Past cancer/ACS ties:** None found. A Pacific Power news release from May 31, 2024 announced $245,000 in Safety/Wellness grants, including programs that "facilitate healthcare for underserved communities." It did not name a cancer grantee. VERIFIED (company release on FlashAlert). The 990-PF grant list is in "Statement 19", an attachment that was not readable, so ACS grants are unconfirmed.
- **Employee giving/matching:** Not found. A search gave no PacifiCorp-specific result. **Could not research.**
- **Grants/contact page:** https://www.pacificpower.net/foundation. Program inbox: pacificorpfoundation@pacificorp.com ("email preferred"). VERIFIED.
- **Suggested Tier:** 2. There is no explicit cancer focus, but the Safety/Wellness cycle names healthcare accessibility and preventative care, and the deadline is verified.
- **ACS angle:** Apply in the Dec 15 Safety/Wellness cycle for a $2,000 to $5,000 project limited to Pacific Power counties in OR or Central/SE WA. Good options are Road to Recovery rides (healthcare access) or a colorectal/lung screening navigation effort in rural service-area counties. Southeastern Idaho could be covered through Rocky Mountain Power.
- **Sources:**
  - VERIFIED: https://www.pacificpower.net/foundation
  - VERIFIED (company release): https://flashalert.net/id/PacificPower/172712
  - VERIFIED (IRS 990-PF render): https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/organizations/943089826
  - UNVERIFIED (snippet repeated the same cycles): grantedai.com listings

## Portland General Electric (PGE) / PGE Foundation

- **HQ:** Portland, OR.
- **Foundation/program:** PGE has two channels. VERIFIED.
  - The PGE Foundation (EIN 93-1138806), with its own website.
  - PGE corporate grants and sponsorships, through a separate portal.
  - Applications to one channel are not considered by the other.
- **Focus areas:**
  - PGE Foundation pillars: Career-Connected Learning, Career Pathways (economic mobility), Creative Expression (narrowed in 2026 and folded into Career-Connected Learning from 2027), and Climate Resilient Communities (a 2026 pilot). **There is no health pillar.** VERIFIED.
  - Corporate grants: "promoting equity in the green economy and engaging communities in initiatives to address the climate crisis." VERIFIED.
- **Typical grant size:** "Average grant amounts... range from $10,000 to $25,000." Climate Resilient Communities is capped at $15,000. VERIFIED. FY2024 grants paid were $1,503,607. VERIFIED (IRS 990-PF via ProPublica).
- **Geographic limits:**
  - Foundation: "Organizations based in Oregon and serving Oregonians with priority to groups in counties where PGE has customers and power generating plants." It must be a 501(c)(3). VERIFIED.
  - Corporate: the applicant must be "located in, and primarily serve communities where PGE provides electrical service or operates power generating facilities." VERIFIED.
  - **Oregon only; WA and ID are not eligible.**
- **How to apply:** Online through Cybergrants, with no paper or email applications. One request per calendar year. Prior grantees must file an impact report first. The application requires the current operating budget, balance sheet and P&L. VERIFIED.
- **Open RFPs/deadlines (12 months):**
  - All posted 2026 foundation windows are closed. VERIFIED:
    - Career-Connected Learning: Nov 20, 2025 to Jan 9, 2026
    - Creative Expression: Feb 23 to Apr 3, 2026
    - Climate Resilient Communities: Mar 23 to May 8, 2026
    - Career Pathways: May 25 to Jul 11, 2026
  - The 2026–27 dates are **not yet posted.** If last year's pattern repeats, Career-Connected Learning would open around late November 2026. That is an inference only and UNVERIFIED.
  - The corporate portal lists no deadlines.
- **Past cancer/ACS ties:** None found. The 990-PF grant list says "full list available upon request." VERIFIED.
- **Employee giving/matching:**
  - PGE runs an annual fall Employee Giving Campaign with a "100% match, from shareholder contributions." The 2025 campaign raised more than $1.79M for over 1,200 Oregon nonprofits and schools. VERIFIED (PGE newsroom, Dec 2, 2025).
  - Double the Donation reports a $2,000 per-employee cap and $200 volunteer grants. UNVERIFIED.
- **Grants/contact page:**
  - Foundation: https://pgefoundation.org/apply-for-funding. The foundation's program-officer email is published on that page.
  - Corporate: https://portlandgeneral.com/about/who-we-are/grants-sponsorships
- **Suggested Tier:** 3. The foundation's pillars don't match ACS's mission, and the corporate priorities are climate and the green economy.
- **ACS angle:** The best near-term play is getting ACS Oregon into the fall Employee Giving Campaign, where gifts are matched 1:1. A workforce-wellness or screening event for PGE employees could lead to a later corporate sponsorship request for an ACS Oregon event.

  - VERIFIED: https://pgefoundation.org/funding-opportunities
  - VERIFIED: https://portlandgeneral.com/about/who-we-are/grants-sponsorships
  - VERIFIED: https://portlandgeneral.com/about/who-we-are/community

## Nike

- **HQ:** Beaverton, OR.
- **Foundation/program:**
  - Nike Community Impact Fund (NCIF), administered by Oregon Community Foundation (OCF). VERIFIED (OCF page).
  - Nike Social & Community Impact (SCI) grantmaking, including the Black Community Commitment. VERIFIED (Nike's grantmaking guide PDF).
  - Nike Foundation and employee giving through "Give Your Best." VERIFIED (about.nike.com).
- **Focus areas:** NCIF supports "youth positive experiences in sport and physical activity." **New for 2026: "100 percent of grant awards support projects that get more kids active through sport or other physical activities."** VERIFIED. SCI is focused on "getting kids active and building inclusive communities." VERIFIED.
- **Typical grant size:** NCIF "Awards typically range from $5,000 to $25,000," with at least $550,000 per year. VERIFIED.
- **Geographic limits:** NCIF covers the Portland metro region, the Northern Willamette Valley and southwest Washington. VERIFIED (OCF page). Most of WA and all of ID are excluded.
- **How to apply:** NCIF applications go through OCF's MyOCF portal; a new nonprofit account takes about one week to set up. VERIFIED. The SCI guide describes Nike-led participatory selection with no open application. VERIFIED (guide).
- **Open RFPs/deadlines (12 months):** The 2026 NCIF cycle ran Jan 8 to Feb 17, 2026 and is now closed. VERIFIED. 2027 dates are not posted. The cycle may reopen around January 2027 (inference, UNVERIFIED). ACS programs would not fit the youth-sport-only criteria anyway.
- **Past cancer/ACS ties:**
  - No Nike corporate cancer grant was found.
  - Co-founder Phil Knight and Penny Knight pledged $2B to the OHSU Knight Cancer Institute in Aug 2025. That is **personal** giving, not Nike corporate giving. UNVERIFIED (news).
- **Employee giving/matching:**
  - Nike's own page says Give Your Best lets "NIKE, Inc. and the Nike Foundation" match employee donations "to schools and charitable organizations," and that volunteers earn "a monetary credit per hour." VERIFIED.
  - The specific amounts are UNVERIFIED and did not appear on the page as read: a 1:1 match up to $25,000 per employee per year, and a $10/hour volunteer credit up to $2,500 per year. These came from a search snippet and Double the Donation.
- **Grants/contact page:**
  - https://oregoncf.org/grants-and-scholarships/grants/nike-community-impact-fund-program. Program inbox: OCF-NikeFund@oregoncf.org (VERIFIED). OCF technical inbox: grants@oregoncf.org.
  - https://about.nike.com/en/mission/initiatives/employee-community-engagement
- **Suggested Tier:** 3. Nike's grantmaking is tied to youth sport.
- **ACS angle:** The realistic routes are employee matching through Give Your Best, plus an ACS-hosted employee wellness or screening session at Nike World Headquarters that brings in volunteers. Physical activity is an ACS cancer-prevention guideline, but that link is too weak for a youth-sport NCIF grant.

## Intel

- **HQ:** Santa Clara, CA. The PNW footprint is Hillsboro, OR, Intel's largest site; the Intel Foundation's filing address is in Hillsboro, OR. VERIFIED (IRS 990-PF / ProPublica).
- **Foundation/program:** Intel Foundation (EIN 94-3092928) and Intel corporate community giving.
- **Focus areas:**
  - The FY2025 990-PF shows grants of $19,223,256. Of that, $12,170,119 went to American Online Giving Foundation (Benevity) for "matching gifts, volunteer matching grants," and $4,093,687 went to UK Online Giving Foundation for the same purpose. Other grants were STEM and education: Project Lead The Way ($1,000,000), Andy Grove scholarships for employees' children ($1,559,450), and ISEF outreach. VERIFIED (IRS 990-PF via ProPublica).
  - The stated foundation focus is STEM equity, technology access, disaster response and amplifying employee generosity. UNVERIFIED: intel.com pages returned HTTP 403 and could not be read.
- **Typical grant size:** Not applicable to ACS. Funding to outside organizations is essentially employee-driven matching.
- **Geographic limits:** Not applicable to unsolicited requests. Matching follows where employees and retirees give.
- **How to apply:** **The Intel Foundation does not accept unsolicited requests.** Its FY2024 and FY2025 990-PFs both have the box checked for "only makes contributions to preselected charitable organizations and does not accept unsolicited requests for funds." VERIFIED.
- **Open RFPs/deadlines (12 months):** None.
- **Past cancer/ACS ties:** None found. Intel Foundation grants to ACS through Benevity matching are likely, but they cannot be confirmed because the 990-PF reports them as one lump sum.
- **Employee giving/matching:** UNVERIFIED (search snippets; intel.com blocked):
  - The foundation matches employee and retiree donations.
  - It gives $10 per volunteer hour after 20 hours at an organization (Intel Involved Matching Grant).
  - Retirees have a combined $1,000 cap.
  - Requests are due within 60 days, with a Dec 31 annual cutoff.

  The scale of matching is VERIFIED through the $16.3M paid to Benevity-affiliated entities in FY2025.
- **Grants/contact page:** https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/support/articles/000054976/programs/intel-corporation.html (Intel's "Does Intel Foundation accept requests for proposals?" FAQ). The page was blocked (403) for this research, so its content is unverified.
- **Suggested Tier:** 3 for grants. The matching channel is meaningful because of the large Hillsboro workforce.
- **ACS angle:** Recruit Intel Hillsboro employees and retirees to give and volunteer with ACS Oregon, for example as Road to Recovery drivers. Once they log 20 or more hours, ACS registered on Benevity can receive Intel volunteer-match dollars. A workforce screening-awareness program could also be offered to Intel's Oregon site.
