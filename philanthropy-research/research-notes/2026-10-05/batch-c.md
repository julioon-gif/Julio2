# Batch C: Idaho, U.S. Bank and Silver Cloud (research date 2026-10-05)

Companies: Micron / Micron Foundation, Idaho Power / IDACORP, J.R. Simplot Company, U.S. Bank / U.S. Bank Foundation, Silver Cloud Inns & Hotels (current ACS account).

**Labels used below.**
- **VERIFIED** means read today on the company's or foundation's own page or PDF, or in the foundation's own IRS 990-PF as rendered by ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer.
- **UNVERIFIED** means it came from a search snippet, a third-party directory or news.
- Grant figures from 990-PFs are what the foundation reported paying in that tax year. They are not a promise of future giving.

**Method notes.**
- Pages were read with curl.
- PDFs were read with pypdf. pdftotext is not installed, so pypdf was installed into the session scratchpad.
- 990-PF grant lists were read from ProPublica's rendered full-text filings (`projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/full_text/<object_id>/IRS990PF`). The raw XML download is blocked by a ProPublica security check.
- 15 of about 45 WebSearch calls were used.

- **HQ:** Boise, ID (VERIFIED: P.O. Box 70, Boise, ID 83707, in the idahopower.com footer)

- **Focus areas:** five categories: **Health and Human Services**, Education, Culture and Arts, Civic and Community, and Environment and Recreation (VERIFIED: guidelines PDF https://docs.idahopower.com/pdfs/AboutUs/CommunityOutreach/CommunityContributionGuidelines.pdf).

  - Priority goes to organizations that serve large numbers of people, have long-term effects, **serve or include Idaho Power employees**, and have broad community support (VERIFIED, guidelines).

- **Typical grant size:** not published. The application asks for the requested amount and the total project budget (VERIFIED). No dollar range was found.

  - **Washington is not eligible.**
  - Applicants must be US 501(c)(3)s (VERIFIED).
- **How to apply:**
  1. Complete the online Community Contribution form (https://tools.idahopower.com/forms/CommunityContribution/Submit).
  2. Attach the 501(c)(3) letter, the latest 990 and sponsorship levels if it is an event.
  3. Expect a response "generally no more than 60 days following submission".

  The older PDF form says to email the package to **Contributions@idahopower.com** (VERIFIED: the PDF form is dated 02/01/2024 and the inbox also appears on the funds-scholarships page). For Employee Community Funds there are separate New Funding and Continued Funding applications. Approved agencies may reapply every two years (VERIFIED).
- **Open RFPs / deadlines (next 12 months):** rolling, with no deadline (VERIFIED). No dated cycle exists.
- **Past cancer / ACS ties:** a 2006 Idaho Business Review column names Idaho Power among the businesses supporting the **Boise Relay For Life** with "significant donations in money, time and effort" (UNVERIFIED (news, 2006): https://idahobusinessreview.com/?p=6055). No recent ACS gift was found.
- **Employee giving / matching:** Employee Community Funds pool employee and retiree donations, and IDACORP "matches much of these contributions" (VERIFIED). The match ratio and cap are not published. Employees also volunteer (Volunteer Spotlight on the site).
- **Grants / contact page:** https://www.idahopower.com/community-safety/community-engagement/funds-scholarships/ ; inbox contributions@idahopower.com (VERIFIED)
- **Suggested Tier:** 2. This is the most actionable open door in batch C: Health and Human Services is an explicit category, applications are rolling, and the past Relay tie is UNVERIFIED.
- **ACS angle:**
  - Apply under Health and Human Services for **Road to Recovery rides and patient navigation for rural southern Idaho and eastern Oregon patients**, who face long drives to Boise or Salt Lake City cancer centers. Mention any Idaho Power employee volunteers, since the form asks and the guidelines favor it.
  - A separate ask: apply to Employee Community Funds for matched employee dollars.

## U.S. Bank / U.S. Bank Foundation (Community Possible)

- **HQ:** Minneapolis, MN (UNVERIFIED this run; this is the company's well-known HQ and was not re-read today).

- **Focus areas:** three pillars:
  - **Work:** small business, workforce education, financial education
  - **Home:** affordable housing, homeownership, neighborhood stability
  - **Play:** arts and culture, learning through play, outdoor places

  It prioritizes low- and moderate-income communities. **Health is not a pillar** (VERIFIED). It also funds American Red Cross chapters by invitation.

- **How to apply:** **by invitation only**, but there is an electronic **Letter of Interest** that new organizations "may submit at any time during the year" ("Submit letter of interest" on the program page) (VERIFIED). Established partners are invited annually by a community affairs manager.
  - Questions: **usbankfoundation@usbank.com** or 855-237-7875 (VERIFIED).
- **Open RFPs / deadlines (next 12 months):** **no fixed grant windows**; the LOI is open year-round (VERIFIED). The "December 31, 2026" date on a university funding calendar is UNVERIFIED and conflicts with usbank.com.
- **Past cancer / ACS ties:** none found (one web search; no ACS–U.S. Bank sponsorship surfaced in WA, OR or ID).
- **Employee giving / matching:**
  - "The U.S. Bank Foundation matches employee charitable contributions up to **$2,500 per year**" in cash and stock to qualified nonprofits (VERIFIED, charitable-giving page).
  - Employees get up to **16 hours of paid volunteer time** a year (VERIFIED).
  - An older third-party form shows a $1,000 cap (outdated, UNVERIFIED: https://doublethedonation.com/forms/us-bank.pdf).
- **Grants / contact page:** https://www.usbank.com/about-us-bank/community/community-possible-grant-program.html ; usbankfoundation@usbank.com
- **Suggested Tier:** 3. Health is outside all three pillars, and events and sponsorships are excluded.
- **ACS angle:** Not a grant fit. The best route is employee engagement: matched gifts up to $2,500 and volunteer hours for Road to Recovery drivers. The only craftable grant tie would be a "Work" pitch, such as health-workforce or patient-navigator career pathways for low- and moderate-income residents, but that is a stretch.
