Scope: companies headquartered in WA, OR, or ID, **plus** companies HQ'd elsewhere with a significant PNW presence.
Per-company notes with every source link: `research-notes/2026-09-28/` (full re-scan) , `research-notes/2026-10-05/` `research-notes/2026-10-12/` and `research-notes/2026-10-19/` (weekly scans). The page-reading helper the scans use is in `tools/readpage.py`.
