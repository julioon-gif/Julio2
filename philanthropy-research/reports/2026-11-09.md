# PNW Corporate Philanthropy Scan: Week of Nov 9, 2026

**6 new companies, 13 changed.** **Milestone: every company in the tracker (105) has now been checked on the funder's own site.** **(V)** means verified on the funder's own page or official filing; **(U)** means unverified.

## Top 3 actions (verified)
1. **Genentech: submit before Nov 26 (V).** Its request system closes then and reopens Jan 4. Requests must arrive at least 60 days before the program starts, and Hillsboro/Portland community programs get extra consideration. https://www.gene.com/good/giving/corporate-giving
2. **Trillium Community Health Plan (Oregon), new: apply for a 2027 sponsorship by Dec 31, 5 p.m. PT (V).** Event sponsorships are $100–$5,000 and program support $250–$3,000, for organizations serving Clackamas, Western Douglas, Lane, Linn, Multnomah or Washington County. Its priorities include prevention and access to care, and it has funded a ride-to-care program, mammography events and tobacco-cessation events. https://www.trilliumohp.com/supporting-oregon-communities/sponsorships.html
3. **Siemens Healthineers, new Tier 1 (V).** Its US community-impact page names the American Cancer Society among the organizations it supports, and says it funds breast, liver and lung cancer screening for the uninsured. Its US giving portal is open to US nonprofits. Requests need at least 45 days' notice and co-funding, and it doesn't fund walks or sporting events. One caution: it exited general-imaging ultrasound in October, which may affect its Issaquah site. https://www.siemens-healthineers.com/en-us/about-us/diversity-inclusion/community-impact

## Changes
- **Alaska Airlines: moved from Not a fit to Tier 2 (V).** Its foundation covers only Alaska and Hawai'i, but its **corporate giving** program accepts event sponsorships and donated flights from nonprofits in WA, OR and ID (plus other states), one time per organization, requested at least 6 weeks ahead. It has donated miles to ACS in Alaska for patient travel. Relay For Life and Making Strides would need a team of Alaska employees.
- **Meta: moved from Not a fit to Tier 3 (V).** Its US data-center grants run under a different program, "The Future is for Everyone Fund," which covers Prineville, OR and Kuna, ID. The 2026 window has closed, and health isn't one of its focus areas.
- **iQ Credit Union, raised to Tier 2 (V).** iQ for Kids takes open applications, and its committee meets in Feb, May, Aug and Nov. It has supported St. Baldrick's, a childhood-cancer charity. Angle: HPV-vaccination outreach in Clark County.
- **Russell Investments, raised to Tier 2 (V).** Health and human services is one of its two giving pillars, and its Spark! program matches employee gifts up to $5,500 a year. There's no public application, so go through employees. Its sale to a B Capital-led group is expected by Q1 2027.
- **Snohomish County PUD: now Not a fit (V).** Its sponsorships must promote utility programs.
- **Smaller corrections (V):**
  - Google has given $35M+ to Oregon since 2004, not $14M.
  - Novo Nordisk's Seattle site is unconfirmed (its labs reportedly closed in 2023).
  - Columbia Bank lets local teams give their own sponsorships, and its inbox is community@Columbiabank.com.
  - Starbucks is employee giving only (up to $1,000 matched).
  - WSECU is still "at capacity"; re-check in January.

## New prospects
- **Siemens Healthineers** (Issaquah, WA site), Tier 1: see above.
- **Trillium Community Health Plan** (Oregon), Tier 2: see above.
- **Rosauers Supermarkets** (Spokane, WA; stores in WA, ID, OR and MT), Tier 2. A 2005 news report says it gave $500K+ to ACS Washington and ran a sales day for ACS (unverified). Check ACS records and pitch a new checkout or sales-day campaign.
- **Coordinated Care** (Washington Medicaid plan), Tier 2: it funded rural mobile mammography in 2023. There's no public application, so this is a relationship ask.

- **Idaho Central Credit Union**, Tier 3: event sponsorships with at least 30 days' notice. It matches employee gifts during Idaho Gives.
