# PNW Corporate Philanthropy Scan: Week of Oct 12, 2026

**0 new companies, 17 changed.** This week finishes the backlog: **all 93 tracked companies are now researched.** **(V)** means verified on the funder's own page or official filing; **(U)** means unverified.

## Top 3 actions (verified)
1. **Walmart Spark Good Local Grants: apply by Nov 30 (V).** Individual Walmart stores, Sam's Clubs and distribution centers each give grants of $250–$5,000 to organizations in their service area. One organization can have up to 25 applications open at once, so ACS could file Road to Recovery or screening-education requests with several WA, OR and ID stores. It first needs a Spark Good account at Walmart.com/nonprofits, verified by Deed. Walmart has been a national ACS partner since 1960 and has given more than $100M (per cancer.org). https://walmart.org/how-we-give/program-guidelines/spark-good-local-grants-guidelines
2. **Kroger Co. Foundation (QFC and Fred Meyer): apply by Dec 4 (V).** QFC is a current account. Pitch cancer-prevention nutrition or screening outreach under its "health & nutrition" focus area. https://www.communitygifts.com
3. **Pacific Power Foundation: apply by Dec 15 (V).** Its Safety/Wellness cycle covers healthcare accessibility and preventive care. Pitch rides or screening navigation in Pacific Power counties. https://www.pacificpower.net/foundation

## Changes this week
- **Albertsons Companies Foundation, downgraded from Tier 1 to Tier 3 (V).** Its website is now "Nourishing Neighbors" and covers hunger only. The regional guidelines that named cancer have been removed, and the only open funding is a hunger-innovation proposal fund. A Safeway checkout campaign would be a separate corporate ask.
- **Walmart, upgraded to Tier 1:** see the top action above.
- **United Health Foundation, upgraded to Tier 1.** It funds cancer-screening access, for example $250K for colorectal and prostate screening. Grants are invitation-only, so contact unitedhealthfoundationinfo@uhg.com to ask about an invitation.
- **Holland America Line, Tier 2 (V).** Nonprofits in health and human services can buy one reduced-fare cruise certificate a year ($700 or $1,000) to auction. Request it at least 90 days before the event. Search snippets also show past ACS "Toast to Life" events and Hope Gala sponsorship in Seattle (unverified).
- **NW Natural, Tier 2 (V).** It takes health and human services grant requests year-round and reviews them quarterly. It covers Western Oregon and Southwest Washington, and won't fund general operations at hospitals.
- **Weyerhaeuser, Tier 2 (V).** It funds human services (not health) in eligible WA and OR counties and Boise. Its 2026 cycle closed Sep 30, and it excludes galas and sponsorships.
- **Current accounts:**
  - **Aon:** it is a 2026 sponsor of an ACS gala in Green Bay, WI (V), which is a precedent for a Seattle ask. It also lists health and human services as a giving priority.
  - **eXp World Holdings:** now renamed AGNT, Inc., with a new CEO since Oct 8 (V). Its charity fund only helps its own agents, so pitch agent teams for Making Strides or a per-home-sale pledge.
  - **Avanade:** new CEO (V). It runs cancer well-being webinars for employees and a holiday giving campaign.
  - **KPFF:** no public giving process, so ask the Seattle and Portland offices for a sponsorship.
- **Employee giving only (Tier 3):** JPMorgan Chase (its employee matching already sent ACS about $46.8K in 2025), Salesforce, Zillow, Nintendo, Comcast, and Les Schwab (donates tires to nonprofit vehicles; the 2027 cycle opens Feb 1).
