## Changes
- **Microsoft, raised to Tier 2 (V).** A new **Community-First Grants** program gives $1M a year to King County nonprofits: 25 grants per cycle, about $20K each. Cycle 1 closed Oct 23; Cycle 2 dates aren't posted, so watch for them. Give Match is now confirmed at 1:1 up to $15K per employee per year, plus $25 per volunteer hour, so make sure ACS is registered for it. https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/corporate-responsibility/topics/community-first-grants
- **Amazon, raised to Tier 2 (V).** AWS Imagine Grant awards go up to $200K, $150K or $50K in cash plus AWS credits, and Breastcancer.org is a past winner. 2027 dates aren't posted (the 2026 round opened Mar 19). A strong fit for a helpline, navigation or ride-matching technology proposal.
- **Boise Cascade, raised to Tier 2 (V).** It runs a yearly St. Jude pediatric cancer campaign ($494K in 2026). Its Idaho HQ takes requests year-round, including for "basic needs." Pitch Road to Recovery and lodging.
- **Auburn Mechanical (current account) (V).** It **does** have a foundation, the Auburn Foundation, with a "Well-Being" area covering wellness programs. Its annual golf classic benefits that foundation. Ask to become a Well-Being partner.
- **Lyell Immunopharma (V).** Its Bothell CAR-T plant is now approved for commercial production, and it holds an employee volunteer month every April. Pitch April 2027 volunteer projects.
- **WaFd Bank, now Not a fit (V).** Its foundation excludes "medical endeavors: fundraisers, research, treatment… medical related events."
- **OnPoint Credit Union, now Not a fit (V).** It explicitly excludes healthcare and medical services.
- **WinCo Foods, now Not a fit.** Its foundation is very small and mainly helps employees in hardship.
- **evo (current account):** its $10M pledge appears only in a 2021 release, and current giving is about $123K a year (V). Better asks are a sun-safety program and staff volunteering as drivers.
- **PSE:** grants go up to $15K (2026 theme: disaster preparedness), and Powerful Partnerships pays $12,500 per partner. Its 2027 round opens Jan 4 (V).
- **Smaller corrections:** REI's HQ may now be Sumner, WA. Gesa Local Heroes details stay unverified because its site blocked the research tool. Submittable has no confirmed Seattle office.
