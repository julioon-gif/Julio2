# PNW Corporate Philanthropy Scan: Week of Oct 26, 2026

**2 new companies, 16 changed.** This week I re-checked 13 entries from the Sep 28 scan on the funders' own pages, added 2 new companies, and found that Walmart extended its grant cycle. **(V)** means verified on the funder's own page or official filing; **(U)** means unverified.

## Top 3 actions (verified)
1. **KeyBank Community Sponsorships: submit by Oct 31 (V).** Requests ($500–$5K) are accepted January through October only, so this week is the last chance for 2026. Eligible counties include King, Pierce, Snohomish, Spokane and Clark in WA, Multnomah and Lane in OR, and Ada in ID. https://www.key.com/about/corporate-responsibility/apply-for-a-grant.html
2. **Genentech: submit before Nov 26 (V).** Its request system closes then and reopens Jan 4. Requests must arrive at least 60 days before the program starts, and Hillsboro/Portland community programs get extra consideration. https://www.gene.com/good/giving/corporate-giving
3. **MolinaCares (Molina Healthcare of Washington): new Tier 1, a relationship ask (V).** It has funded Washington breast-cancer screening, including $75K for mobile mammography on the Kitsap Peninsula in 2026. There's no public application, so propose a screening and navigation partnership for rural Medicaid members. https://molinacares.com/molinacares-grants-75000-expand-mobile-mammography-access-on-the-kitsap-peninsula/

## Changes
- **Walmart:** Cycle 3 now closes **Dec 31**, not Nov 30 (V).
- **McKinstry (current account), raised to Tier 1.** Its foundation's tax filings show it gave ACS $300–$500 a year in 2023–25 (V), and "Health & Science" is a real grant category. Grants go only to organizations it picks, so grow ACS as an employee "People's Choice" pick and log ACS volunteer hours for its Impact Fund match. Then ask the trustees for a larger gift.
- **Molina, raised to Tier 1:** see above.
- **Brighton Jones (current account):** its foundation lists access to healthcare as a pillar (V). It has given $10K to the Rivkin Center for Ovarian Cancer, so a staff-selected grant of about $10K is realistic.
- **Superfeet (current account):** its Local Grants ($2.5K–$10K) cover physical and mental health care, and an employee committee picks them (V). The 2026 window (Aug 3–21) has closed. 2027 is likely August, so recruit employee drivers first.
- **Expeditors:** HQ is **Bellevue** (V). It funds disease research, and a giving committee meets twice a year with no public application. It runs a 50% employee match campaign.
- **Daimler Truck NA:** it **does** have an application, through its Benevity "Daimler Truck Cares" form (V).
- **Community Health Plan of Washington:** its 2025 grants included a Spokane cancer support group and a medical-travel charity (V). There's no public application.
- **Lowered to Tier 3:**
  - T-Mobile: homelessness and digital access only, and the 2026 cycle has closed.
  - Expedia: its Made to Travel Fund is now listed as a past initiative.
  - Stanley 1913: no health causes.
  - PCC: its site is blocked, and the round-up funds local farms.
  - Sana Biotechnology: no giving program.

## New prospects
- **First Interstate Bank** (Billings, MT, with branches in OR, WA and ID), Tier 2: "Health" is a giving category, and it gave $7.2M in 2025. Foundation grants start at $2,500 with a local-branch recommendation, applications go through YourCause, and it reviews monthly (V).
- **STCU** (Spokane), Tier 3: covers education, arts and economic development across eastern WA and north Idaho. Sponsorship requests are due 4–6 months before the event.
