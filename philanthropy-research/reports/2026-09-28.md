# Corporate Philanthropy Scan (Puget Sound scope) — Week of Sep 28, 2026

**First full scan: 2 new companies, 11 researched for the first time.** Everything below comes from public sources, and each item links to its source. Anything I couldn't confirm on the funder's own site is marked "verify".

## Top 3 actions this week
1. **Costco (Issaquah): apply now through its rolling Benevity application.** It funds health & human services. Pitch: Road to Recovery rides and Hope Lodge nights for patients in the communities where Costco has warehouses. Costco allows about 3 months' lead time and reviews in 4–6 weeks (third-party summary, verify on costco.com). Next step: confirm the guidelines at https://costco.com/charitable-giving.html and draft a request that stays under the 10% share of program budget Costco allows.
2. **Kaiser Permanente Washington and Premera: build the relationship.** Both give by invitation only and fund access to care and health equity. Pitch: screening access, HPV outreach, and patient navigation for underserved patients. Next step: find a warm introduction to each community-health team, for example through a board member, volunteer or health-system partner.
3. **BECU: line up a member nomination for the 2027 People Helping People Awards.** Its named focus areas include patient support and illness prevention/cure ($15K–$50K, WA eligible). 2026 nominations are closed. Next step: find BECU members among ACS volunteers or staff who could nominate ACS when the 2027 window opens (date not yet published).

## Upcoming deadlines (next 90 days)

- Just beyond the window: **Nordstrom Community Grants, Jan 1–31, 2027** (third-party listing, verify). Weak fit.

## Researched for the first time
- **Costco (Tier 2, Prospect):** rolling Benevity application; health & human services. https://costco.com/charitable-giving.html
- **T-Mobile (Tier 2, Prospect):** HQ Grants for Greater Seattle, focused on digital equity, with about 20% going to homelessness. Pitch: connecting patients to the helpline, telehealth and virtual navigation. The application window isn't published. https://www.t-mobile.com/responsibility/community/hq-grants
- **Premera (Tier 2, Watching):** Social Impact program funds behavioral health and health equity. It gives by invitation only and isn't accepting proposals. https://wellbeyond.premera.com/social-impact/
- **Providence (Tier 2, Watching):** Providence Swedish's 2025–27 community health plan includes mobile mammography for underserved patients. Better as a delivery partner than as a funder. https://blog.swedish.org/community/swedish-releases-community-health-improvement-plan-address-community-health-needs-king-county
- **Amazon (Tier 3):** Puget Sound giving goes mostly to housing and food. The AWS Imagine Grant (technology for nonprofits) could fund helpline or navigation technology, but the 2026 round closed Jun 5. https://aws.amazon.com/government-education/nonprofits/aws-imagine-grant-program
- **Microsoft (Tier 3):** grants are by invitation only, with no unsolicited proposals. Employee matching gifts run year-round, so the angle is employee giving drives. https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/corporate-responsibility/philanthropies/employee-engagement
- **Nordstrom (Tier 3):** youth, families and basic needs, $5K–$20K, window Jan 2027. https://www.nordstrom.com/browse/impact/people/our-communities/community-grants
- **Puget Sound Energy (Tier 3):** PSE Foundation funds food security, shelter, literacy and nonprofit capacity. Shelter → Hope Lodge is a stretch. https://www.psefoundation.org/
- **WaFd Bank (Tier 3):** micro grants for low- and moderate-income communities, rolling. https://www.instrumentl.com/grants/washington-federal-foundation-grant-ofskagv
- **Alaska Air Group (Not a fit):** its foundation serves Alaska and Hawai'i only and funds culture, place and language. https://news.alaskaair.com/community/new-alaska-airlines-hawaiian-airlines-foundation-to-strengthen-and-support-alaska-and-hawaii-communities/
- **Starbucks (Not a fit):** Neighborhood Grants go to employee-nominated grassroots organizations ($1K–$10K). https://about.starbucks.com/neighborhood-grants/
