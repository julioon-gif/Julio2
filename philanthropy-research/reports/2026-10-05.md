# PNW Corporate Philanthropy Scan: Week of Oct 5, 2026

**0 new companies, 21 changed.** This week I researched 15 companies that hadn't been researched yet and re-checked 6 of last week's leads. This is the first run with funder websites readable, so facts are now marked **(V)**, verified on the funder's own page or official filing, or **(U)**, unverified.

## Top 3 actions (all verified on the funder's own page)
1. **Pacific Power Foundation: apply by Dec 15.** Its Safety/Wellness cycle covers "healthcare accessibility" and "wellness and preventative care." Pitch Road to Recovery rides or rural screening navigation in Pacific Power service counties (parts of OR and central/SE WA). Create a portal account first; approval takes up to 2 business days. https://www.pacificpower.net/foundation
2. **Kroger Co. Foundation (QFC and Fred Meyer): apply by Dec 4.** QFC is a current ACS account. The foundation's grants are quarterly through communitygifts.com. Its priority is ending hunger and food waste, but "health & nutrition" is one of its focus areas, so pitch cancer-prevention nutrition or screening outreach. The Kroger Health president sits on the foundation board, which makes a natural internal sponsor. https://www.communitygifts.com
3. **Silver Cloud Hotels (current account): ask for patient room nights.** Its Lake Union hotel is on the same block as Fred Hutch, and its Broadway hotel is across from Swedish. The founders' Silver Cloud Foundation gave **$15,000 to ACS in 2023** and gives heavily to cancer causes (Fred Hutch, Virginia Mason's new Weymouth Oncology Floor). It gives only to charities it selects, so go through the existing relationship.

## Corrections to last week
- **KeyBank:** grant inquiries (the "Expression of Interest") are accepted **any time**, with a reply within 90 days. The October cutoff applies only to **Community Sponsorships**.
- **BECU:** 2026 nominations ran **Apr 6–24**, not June. The 2027 window is likely around April but isn't posted.
- **Costco:** the "no transportation or research" rule reported by third-party sites is **not on Costco's own FAQ**. What Costco's FAQ does say: rolling requests, one per fiscal year (Sep–Aug), no more than 10% of the program budget, and a 4–6 week review.
- **Cambia Health Foundation:** its 2022–26 plan prioritizes **behavioral health**, not cancer. Reframe the ask around mental-health support for cancer patients and caregivers.
- **PacificSource Foundation:** all three 2026 cycles have closed (the last letter-of-inquiry deadline was Jul 24). 2027 dates aren't posted.
- **Mechanics Bank (formerly HomeStreet), a current account:** the HomeStreet Foundation appears to have dissolved (its last IRS filing shows liquidation; unverified). Mechanics' giving pledge is California-only. Treat this account as at risk.

  - **Idaho Power:** takes health and human services requests on a rolling basis and replies in about 60 days. Organizations in its Idaho and Oregon service area only (verified).
