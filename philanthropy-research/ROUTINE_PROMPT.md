# Routine prompt (current weekly routine text)

Schedule: Mondays 9:00 AM America/New_York · Repository: julioon-gif/Julio2 (branch main) · Connector: Gmail

---

Weekly run: Pacific Northwest Corporate Philanthropy Scan for the American Cancer Society (ACS). Goal: find corporations headquartered in Washington, Oregon, or Idaho, or HQ'd elsewhere with a significant PNW market presence (and their foundations), whose philanthropy could fund ACS, directly or through a mission tie we can craft, and email a report of what is NEW or CHANGED since last week.

1. Repo: use julioon-gif/Julio2 at /home/user/julio2 on branch `main`. If that folder is missing, attach it with the `add_repo` tool (owner julioon-gif, repo Julio2, access push) and clone it as the tool instructs. Then run `git -C /home/user/julio2 pull origin main`. Read philanthropy-research/METHODOLOGY.md (follow it), philanthropy-research/prospects.md (last week's state), and the newest file in philanthropy-research/reports/.
2. DEEP research. Be exhaustive, not quick. Scope has two groups (see METHODOLOGY.md): (a) companies HQ'd in WA, OR, or ID; (b) companies HQ'd elsewhere with a significant PNW presence (major office, R&D site, plant, regional HQ, large workforce, branch network), e.g. Pfizer, Bank of America, KeyBank, Wells Fargo, Chase, U.S. Bank, Boeing, Google, Meta, Intel. Match against all four ACS areas: patient support (Road to Recovery, Hope Lodge, helpline/navigation), cancer research, prevention and screening (HPV, lung/colorectal screening, tobacco cessation), and health equity and workforce wellness.
   - Fully research 12–15 tracker rows per run, mixing both groups and rotating across WA, OR, and ID. Prioritize rows with status `Needs research` or `Current account – expand (needs research)`, then the oldest `Last checked` dates. Re-verify any deadline within 60 days.
   - Search budget: the session has a limited number of web searches (about 200 per run, shared with any subagents), and many company sites are blocked for direct fetching. Budget roughly 10 searches per company, and use at most 3 subagents. If searches run out, mark the unfinished rows `Needs research`, say so in the report, and still finish steps 3–6.
   - Go deep on each company with multiple independent sources: its foundation, CSR and community pages; newsroom and ESG/impact report; 990-PF grant lists (ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer, Candid); local news; ACS and ACS CAN event sponsor lists (Relay For Life, Making Strides, galas); and national ACS partner pages. For presence companies, cover BOTH the PNW-specific giving (regional foundation or grant cycles, market presidents, community-relations leads, employee giving and matching) AND the national programs (national RFPs, existing ACS partnerships, cause marketing).
   - To keep the depth up, split the batch across up to 3 parallel research subagents (Agent tool), 4–5 companies each, each told to return sourced facts only and to save its notes to philanthropy-research/research-notes/YYYY-MM-DD/. Then verify key facts, such as deadlines, amounts and ACS ties, before recording them.
   - Discover companies not yet in the tracker: oncology/biotech and pharma with PNW sites, national and regional banks and credit unions, utilities, health insurers, grocers, manufacturers, tech companies with PNW offices, mid-size private companies, and new corporate foundations. Sources: Puget Sound Business Journal, Portland Business Journal, The Oregonian, Idaho Business Review, Idaho Statesman, Spokesman-Review, GeekWire, Philanthropy Northwest, and state largest-employer lists.
   - For each company, record the foundation or program, focus areas, typical grant size, geographic limits, how to apply, open RFPs and deadlines, past cancer or ACS gifts, and the public grants contact page. Assign a Tier (1 direct / 2 craftable / 3 long shot); for Tier 2, write the specific ACS angle. Every claim needs a source URL; mark anything else "unverified". Never invent amounts, deadlines, or contacts.
   - VERIFICATION GATE (see METHODOLOGY.md rules): only put an item in the email's Top actions or Upcoming deadlines if its date, amount and eligible geography (WA/OR/ID, US nonprofits) were read on the funder's own page or official PDF this run. Otherwise label it "UNVERIFIED — confirm on funder site" and list it separately. Search snippets and grant directories are leads, not facts.
3. Compare against the tracker: new companies, new or changed deadlines or RFPs, opened cycles, new cancer-related grants, tier changes, and program or leadership changes.
4. Update prospects.md: fill in researched rows, add new ones with the HQ as "City, ST", set Last checked to today's date, and sort by Tier, then Company. Write philanthropy-research/reports/YYYY-MM-DD.md. Commit "Weekly scan YYYY-MM-DD: N new, M changed" and run `git -C /home/user/julio2 push origin main`, retrying up to 4 times on network errors. Do not open a PR.
5. Email julio.rios@cancer.org with the Gmail connector's send_message tool (htmlBody plus a plain-text body). Subject: "PNW Corporate Philanthropy Scan — Week of <Mon DD, YYYY>: <N> new, <M> changed". Body sections: Top 3 actions this week (with deadline and next step); Upcoming deadlines in the next 90 days (company, program, date, link); New prospects (company, HQ and PNW footprint, foundation, tier, ACS angle, typical grant, how to apply, sources); Changes to existing prospects; Pipeline snapshot (counts by tier, status, state, and HQ vs. presence, plus researched vs. remaining); links to the tracker https://github.com/julioon-gif/Julio2/blob/main/philanthropy-research/prospects.md and to this week's report https://github.com/julioon-gif/Julio2/blob/main/philanthropy-research/reports/YYYY-MM-DD.md. If nothing changed, still send a short email with the deadlines and the snapshot.
6. Reply here with a short summary: counts, the commit hash, and whether the email was sent. If any step failed, say so on the first line and include the full email body.
