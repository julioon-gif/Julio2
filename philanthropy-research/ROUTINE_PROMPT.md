Weekly run: Pacific Northwest Corporate Philanthropy Scan for the American Cancer Society (ACS). Goal: find corporations headquartered in Washington, Oregon, or Idaho (and their foundations) whose philanthropy could fund ACS, directly or through a mission tie we can craft, and email a report of what is NEW or CHANGED since last week.

1. Repo: use julioon-gif/Julio2 at /home/user/julio2 on branch `main`. If that folder is missing, attach it with the `add_repo` tool (owner julioon-gif, repo Julio2, access push) and clone it as the tool instructs. Then run `git -C /home/user/julio2 pull origin main`. Read philanthropy-research/METHODOLOGY.md (follow it), philanthropy-research/prospects.md (last week's state), and the newest file in philanthropy-research/reports/.
2. Deep research with web search and fetch. Scope: companies HQ'd in WA, OR, or ID, matched against all four ACS areas: patient support (Road to Recovery, Hope Lodge, helpline/navigation), cancer research, prevention and screening (HPV, lung/colorectal screening, tobacco cessation), and health equity and workforce wellness. Fully research 8–10 tracker rows per run. Prioritize `Not yet researched` rows, then the oldest `Last checked` dates, and rotate across WA, OR, and ID so each state gets coverage. Re-verify any deadline within 60 days. Discover companies not yet in the tracker (oncology/biotech, banks, credit unions, utilities, health insurers, grocers, manufacturers, mid-size private companies, new corporate foundations) using Puget Sound Business Journal, Portland Business Journal, The Oregonian, Idaho Business Review, Idaho Statesman, Spokesman-Review, GeekWire, Philanthropy Northwest, 990-PF filings (ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer), and company community-impact pages. For each company, record the foundation or program, focus areas, typical grant size, geographic limits, how to apply, open RFPs and deadlines, past cancer or ACS gifts, and the public grants contact page. Assign a Tier (1 direct / 2 craftable / 3 long shot); for Tier 2, write the specific ACS angle. Every claim needs a source URL; mark anything else "unverified". Never invent amounts, deadlines, or contacts.
