# Weekly Pacific Northwest Corporate Philanthropy Scan — Methodology

Purpose: every Monday, find Pacific Northwest corporations (and their foundations)
whose giving could fund the **American Cancer Society (ACS)**, either directly or through
a mission tie we can credibly craft, and report only what is **new or changed** since last week.

## Geography (in scope)
Two groups of companies are in scope:
1. **PNW-headquartered:** companies HQ'd in **Washington, Oregon, or Idaho**
   (e.g., Seattle/Puget Sound, Spokane, Tri-Cities, Vancouver WA; Portland metro, Beaverton, Hillsboro, Salem, Eugene, Bend;
   Boise, Meridian, Nampa, Idaho Falls, Coeur d'Alene).
2. **PNW market presence:** companies HQ'd elsewhere with a **significant presence in WA, OR, or ID**, such as a major office,
   R&D site, plant, regional HQ, large workforce, branch network, or big customer base.
   Examples: Pfizer (Bothell R&D), Bank of America, KeyBank, Wells Fargo, JPMorgan Chase, U.S. Bank, Boeing, Google, Meta, Intel (Hillsboro).
   For these, research **both** the regional giving (market presidents, regional foundations, local grant cycles, employee
   giving and matching) **and** the national programs (national foundation RFPs, existing national ACS partnerships, cause marketing).
   A national partnership with ACS makes a company Tier 1 even if the regional giving is unclear.

Record HQ as "City, ST". For presence companies, add the PNW footprint in parentheses, e.g. "New York, NY (PNW: Bothell R&D site)".

## ACS programs to match against
1. **Patient support** — Road to Recovery (rides), Hope Lodge (lodging), 24/7 helpline / navigation
2. **Cancer research** — research grants, early-career investigators (Fred Hutch–adjacent ecosystem)
3. **Prevention & screening** — HPV vaccination, lung & colorectal screening, tobacco cessation
4. **Health equity & workforce** — underserved/rural access, employee wellness, cause marketing

## Fit tiers
- **Tier 1 — Direct:** funder explicitly supports cancer, oncology, patient services, or has given to ACS before.
- **Tier 2 — Craftable:** health, health equity, transportation access, housing, community wellbeing,
  STEM/research, employee wellness, rural access. ACS fits with a clear story. State the angle explicitly.
- **Tier 3 — Long shot:** broad community giving or matching-gift/volunteer-grant programs only.

## Sources to check
- Company foundation / community-impact / ESG pages and newsroom
- IRS Form 990-PF filings (ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer, Candid/GuideStar) for grantee lists and totals
- Open RFPs, grant cycles, LOI deadlines; employee matching-gift and volunteer-grant programs
- Local news for new foundations, gifts, leadership changes: Puget Sound Business Journal, Seattle Times, GeekWire, Spokesman-Review,
  Portland Business Journal, The Oregonian, Idaho Business Review, Idaho Statesman
- Philanthropy Northwest, Seattle Foundation, Oregon Community Foundation, Idaho Community Foundation
- Past ACS / ACS CAN sponsorships (Relay For Life, Making Strides, gala sponsors) and ACS national corporate partner pages
- Regional market leadership (e.g., bank market presidents) and regional CSR/community-relations teams for presence companies

## Rules
- **Verification before action.** A deadline, amount or eligibility rule may appear in "Top actions" or "Upcoming deadlines" only if it was read on the funder's own page (or its official PDF) in this run. If the page couldn't be opened, label the item "UNVERIFIED — confirm on funder site" and keep it out of the Top actions.
- **Always check eligible geography.** Confirm the program covers WA, OR or ID (and US nonprofits) before listing it. Programs of global companies often run separate cycles by country.
- **Search snippets and third-party grant directories are leads, not facts.** Never state them as confirmed.
- Every factual claim needs a source URL. If a fact can't be verified, mark it "unverified". Never invent deadlines, amounts, or contacts.
- Contacts: list only publicly posted roles and generic grant inboxes. Don't guess personal emails.
- "New or changed" means: a newly identified prospect, a new or changed deadline or RFP, a new grant cycle,
  a newly found cancer-related grant, a leadership or program change, or a tier change.
